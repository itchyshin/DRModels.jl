- **Independent intercept-plus-slope random effects, `(1 | g) + (0 + x | g) [+ (0 + x2 | g) ...]`, now fit on the Poisson, NegBinomial2, Binomial, Beta, LogNormal and Tweedie mean routes, and `sd(g, coef(.)) ~ ...` formulas on those terms now fit on Poisson, NegBinomial2, Binomial (when some term has no formula) and Gamma.** These shapes were refused with "supports multiple random effects only as crossed/nested intercepts" (Gamma and Student already took the shape without formulas). The fits use the dense TMB-convention Laplace route of Gamma and Student, tagged `marginal = :Laplace`; `:AGHQ`, `:VA` and REML stay refused, and a correlated `(1 + x | g)` beside another term stays refused. LogNormal uses the exact Gaussian marginal of `log(y)`. Slope-only `(0 + x | g)` also reaches the same route on the families that refused it. Against drmTMB 0.8.1 (ML, committed gap-map data and reference values in `test/fixtures/nongauss_indep/`), 75 of the 96 cells drmTMB fits agree to logLik 1e-4, fixed effects 1e-3 and log SD 5e-3 (the 74 away from the Binomial q4 boundary to logLik 8e-9, fixed effects 2e-6, log SD 4e-7; these maxima come from `tools/nongauss_indep_compare.jl`, whose table is not committed, and the test asserts only the limits). The 21 that do not are in four groups, none changed here. Binomial `sd-first` q2 and q4 stop on a flat ridge 0.44 logLik below drmTMB's boundary value (drmTMB flags those fits non-converged; a restart from four alternative starts did not reach it). The older per-group-quadrature SD routes (single slope with `sd()`, Binomial with a formula on every term) differ from drmTMB's Laplace logLik by 0.03 to 0.99, and the Tweedie slope-only GHQ route by 0.03 to 0.07. drmTMB 0.8.1 errors on 40 further cells (`sd()` formulas on independent terms of Beta, Tweedie, LogNormal, Student, and Gamma with three or more terms): Beta and Tweedie stay refused as in R, the others fit with no reference. `sd(g) ~ ...` on crossed intercepts `(1 | g) + (1 | g2)` also fits now on Poisson, LogNormal and Student; those crossed cells, and the slope-only cells of Binomial, Gamma, Student and LogNormal, are compared with drmTMB only by `tools/nongauss_indep_compare.jl`, not by a test. The agreement rule has a boundary exemption added after it was written: a log SD below -6 in either fit skips the SD criterion for that cell only. In the 3008-cell gap map this flips 49 cells to agreement with drmTMB 0.8.1, adds 16 fits where drmTMB errors, and regresses none.
