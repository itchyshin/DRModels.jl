- **Beta `(1 | g)` `marginal = :Laplace` no longer returns the `-1e18` failed-fit placeholder on a good interior optimum, and its Wald SEs are no longer NaN** (Refs #1093). The shared sparse-Laplace mode finder rejected every backtracking step at its 1e-13 tolerance because the decrease fell below floating-point resolution, so it reported failure at a point that was already the mode. It now uses a rounding-tolerant acceptance test and takes the Newton step once the step is tiny and the Hessian is positive definite. `deviance(fit)` now returns `NaN` with a warning on a sentinel fit, like `aic`/`bic`/`aicc`.
