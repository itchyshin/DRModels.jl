- **Ordinary `(1 | g)` profile CIs at the SD floor are `[0, u]`, never a throw or `(-Inf, Inf)` (#1117).** When the lower arm of a `:resd` profile reaches the numerical log-SD floor without an LR crossing, `confint(..., method = :profile)` now reports the SD-scale boundary interval `[0, u]` (drmTMB `profile.boundary`). A pseudo-inverse SE of ~1e-19 no longer seeds a microscopic bracket that never leaves the floor. Interior random-effect SDs stay on the working `log σ_b` scale. Percentile bootstrap under-coverage of the sigma intercept (#1119) is a methods choice, not changed here.
