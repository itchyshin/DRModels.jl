- **Tweedie crossed random intercepts now report finite standard errors.** For `(1 | g) + (1 | g2)` on `Tweedie()`, `stderror(fit)` used to return `Inf` for every coefficient on a converged fit, because the inner fits hold the power fixed and the stored covariance was all `NaN`. The route now takes the standard errors from one joint finite-difference Hessian of the same Laplace objective over the mean and sigma coefficients, both log SDs and the power. Point estimates and the log-likelihood are unchanged. On the committed data the log-likelihood agrees with drmTMB 0.8.1 to 1e-4 and the standard errors to 1e-3 relative (`test/test_tweedie_crossed_se.jl`). A log SD stuck at its floor is held fixed and reported, as on the other crossed routes. Computing the standard errors adds time: about 16 s for one fit in a loaded session.
