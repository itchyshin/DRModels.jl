- **`fitted(fit)` is now conditional for a `LogNormal` `sigma ~ ... + (1 | g)` fit and for a Gaussian mean `(1 | g)` with a `sigma` `(1 | h)` on another group.** Both fits used to refuse the conditional default because the fitted random-effect modes were not kept. They are now recorded at the fitted parameters, so `fitted(fit)` returns `exp(μ + σ̂ᵢ²/2)` with the conditional `σ̂ᵢ` for the `LogNormal` fit, and the fixed part plus the mean-effect mode for the Gaussian fit, as drmTMB's `fitted()` does (checked against drmTMB 0.8.1 to 1e-6, and against a direct maximisation of the joint density over the random effects). `fitted(fit; re = :zero)` is unchanged. Estimates, `loglik` and `vcov` are unchanged. Other routes that keep no modes still refuse the default.
