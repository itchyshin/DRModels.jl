- **Temporal AR1 and OU random effects on the Gaussian mean: `temporal(1 | id, time, ar1)` and `temporal(1 | id, time, ou)` (D-310; twin of drmTMB's `temporal(1 | id, time = occ, structure = "ar1" | "ou")`, drmTMB#1302).** Each series `id` carries a stationary unit-variance chain at its observed times, scaled by a process SD and added to the mean; AR1 uses integer occasions with real gaps (`φ^gap`, `φ = tanh θ`, may be negative), OU uses numeric elapsed time (`exp(−λ Δt)`, `λ = exp θ`). The exact Gaussian marginal is evaluated per series with tridiagonal algebra in O(n) and is checked against a dense `MvNormal` oracle to 1e-10. An ordinary `(1 | id)` on the same id may be added, as in drmTMB. `temporal_parameters(fit)` reports the process SD, `phi` or `decay`, the residual SD and the stable-intercept SD. `fitted()` is population-level (drmTMB's is conditional; the conditional temporal effects are in `ranef(fit)`); `simulate()` and `bootstrap_ci` draw a fresh temporal chain per series, as drmTMB's `simulate()`. `@formula` cannot parse keyword arguments, so the Julia spelling is positional; `drm_bridge` accepts drmTMB's keyword spelling. Wave 1 is Gaussian, `sigma ~ 1`, ML only: other families, a temporal term on `sigma`, slopes, labelled bars, REML, bivariate models, a second temporal or another structured term, missing responses and duplicate `(id, time)` rows are refused by name. drmTMB parity cells are not yet included; the fixtures for them are in `test/fixtures/temporal/`.
