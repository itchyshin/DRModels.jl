- **Fix: `Binomial()` (and `BetaBinomial()`) fits no longer stop on a flat clamp plateau and report a garbage "converged" answer.** The objective clamped the logit linear predictor to ±15 (±30 for the BetaBinomial fixed-effect route) *inside* the log-likelihood, which makes it exactly flat wherever any |η| exceeds the clamp. An L-BFGS step that overshot there (common with a wide covariate, e.g. `sd(x) ≳ 5`) stalled on the plateau with zero gradient and the fit reported `converged = true` with a slope in the tens or hundreds and a log-likelihood near −15 (e.g. 22 non-separated Bernoulli rows: slope 50.8, loglik −15.0 versus the true MLE slope 0.70, loglik −5.56). The Binomial log-likelihood is now the unclamped, numerically stable `k·η − n·log1pexp(η) + log C(n,k)`; the BetaBinomial uses an unclamped logit-parameterised log-pmf (overflow guard at |η| = 700 only). Fits that never leave the old ±15 band are unchanged (coefficients and log-likelihood agree with the previous release to ~1e-12 on the existing fixtures). Two supporting changes make the unclamped fit robust: the `(1 | g)` / `(1 + x | g)` quadrature routes map a non-finite objective probe to the repo's `1e18` failed-fit sentinel so the line search backtracks instead of throwing, and a non-finite AD Hessian at a runaway optimum there is reported as the NaN-covariance convention (standard errors `Inf`, with a warning) instead of throwing a LAPACK error. Completely separated data still terminates and returns a fit (the slope drifts to a large value; the separation guard in #910 still applies).
