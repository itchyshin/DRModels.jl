- **`offset()` holds a fixed coefficient of 1, or the fit refuses (#938).** `offset(z)` was silently estimated as a free coefficient on every route except fixed-effects `Poisson()`, which fitted a different model with no warning (Owls NB2 `(1 | nest)`: offset coefficient 0.65; Owls Poisson `(1 | nest)` under `marginal = :Laplace`: 0.54; Owls NB2 with `sigma ~ Food`: 0.73). The offset is now applied with its coefficient fixed at 1, never as a design column or parameter, for `NegBinomial2()` fixed effects (including `sigma ~ x`) and `(1 | g)` (`:LA` and `:Laplace`), `Poisson()` `(1 | g)` under `:Laplace`, and Gaussian identity (the fit of `y - offset`, offset added back to the fitted means). Every other route (NB2 VA, crossed, slopes, structured, `zi`/`hu`; `:Laplace` Gamma, Beta, Binomial; an offset in `sigma`, `nu`, `zi` or `hu`; every other family) now throws an `ArgumentError` instead. `predict(fit, newdata)` uses the new offset.
