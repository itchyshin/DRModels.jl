- **`offset(z)` in the mean formula now works for NegBinomial2 and every other family, with its coefficient fixed at 1, as in drmTMB (#938).** Before #1057 a non-Poisson `offset(z)` was silently estimated as a free coefficient; #1057 refused it. Now every univariate family applies it on the fixed-effects mean (Gaussian by fitting `y - z` and adding `z` back to the fitted means), NegBinomial2 also with `zi`/`hu` and with one ordinary `(1 | g)` (`marginal = :LA` or `:Laplace`), and Gamma and Beta also with one `(1 | g)` under `marginal = :Laplace`. Fitted means, `predict(fit, newdata)` (which needs the offset column) and `simulate` carry the offset. Other routes (crossed, slope, structured, `marginal = :VA`, coupled location-scale, Gaussian random effects or missing responses) refuse it by name, and an offset in `sigma` or any other non-mean formula stays refused, as drmTMB refuses it. The R bridge (`drm_bridge`) now passes `offset(...)` through instead of refusing it. On glmmTMB's Owls data, NB2 with `offset(log(BroodSize))` matches drmTMB 8aafcc2c in logLik to 1e-5 (fixed effects with `sigma ~ 1` and `sigma ~ Food`, and `(1 | Nest)` under `marginal = :Laplace`).
