- **New: a variance-component boundary warning for Gaussian fits with a grouped / structured random effect and `sigma ~ 1` (#724, #697).** When the structured term absorbs essentially all the variance (residual SD below `1e-3` of the SD of the random-effect BLUPs, i.e. a residual variance share below about `1e-6`) or the structured SD collapses to 0 relative to the residual, `drm` now warns and names the cause, and `check_drm(fit).variance_boundary` carries the flags (`residual_at_boundary`, `structured_at_boundary`, ratios, `one_obs_per_group`; `nothing` where the diagnostic is not defined). The residual `σ̂` of such a fit is an optimiser-stopping artefact, not an estimate (the likelihood is increasing in `σ²` from 0, so the log-scale gradient vanishes like `σ²`), so it is not twin-stable between engines (geiger::carnivores, n = 16: drmTMB 4.3e-5, DRModels.jl 3.1e-4, with `μ`, log-likelihood and the structured SD equal to 1e-8); compare `μ`, log-likelihood and the structured SD, not `σ`. Bootstrap refits stay quiet. The trigger is calibrated on 48 simulated phylogenetic fits and the full test suite (boundary fits `≤ 4.4e-5`, interior fits `≥ 2.2e-2`). Healthy fits are unchanged apart from `check_drm` gaining the `variance_boundary` field. With one observation per group, `σ_a` and `σ_e` are separated only by the off-diagonal structure of `A` (singular exactly for a star tree); the warning says so when that design collapses (see the location-scale-scale tutorial).
- **New: covariate-conditional repeatability / phylogenetic share for location-scale-scale fits (#694).** With `sd(id) ~ z` and `sigma ~ z`, repeatability is a function of the covariates, `R(z) = σ_b(z)² / (σ_b(z)² + σ_e(z)²) = logistic(2(α'z − γ'z))`, not a number. `repeatability(fit, newdata)`, `icc(fit, newdata)` (iid `sd`) and `heritability(fit, newdata)` (`sd(g, phylogenetic)`: `h²(z) = σ_a(z)² / (σ_a(z)² + σ_e(z)²)`) now return `R(z)` per row with a Wald-on-logit interval (the logit is linear in the coefficients; the joint `vcov` of the `sd` and `sigma` blocks is used). The single-argument forms still refuse these fits, and the refusal now states the estimand and points to the two-argument form. No behaviour change for fits without `sd()` submodels.
