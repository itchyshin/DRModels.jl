- **Gaussian `phylo(1 | g)` quantile residuals are now the population-level PIT (#995, #996).** The sparse and conjugate-EM routes store the BLUP-conditional mean in `fitted()`, and the quantile residual integrated σ_b around that mean, counting the random effect twice, and used σ_b² for every tip although a tip's prior variance is σ_b²·C_ii on a raw branch-length tree. `residuals(fit; type = :quantile)` now centres on the fixed-effect mean `Xβ̂` and uses σ_b²·C_ii per tip, evaluated in closed form, so it equals `(y − Xβ̂) / √(σ̂_e² + σ̂_b² C_ii)` to rounding (about 1e-15), and no longer warns. The 32-node Gauss–Hermite mixture used for other random intercepts is not accurate enough here: its residual error is 6e-3 at σ̂_b √C_ii / σ̂_e = 4 and 0.12 at 10. The dense structured fallback keeps the warning. `fit.ranef` on these fits is now a dict-like `LocOnlyRanef` (same `group => BLUPs` entries); the EM route now stores its BLUPs too.
- **The per-tip variance C_ii behind that residual costs O(p) (#995).** It is the diagonal of the root-conditioned tree covariance, now read from a Takahashi selected inverse instead of one sparse solve per tip, and is cached on the fit. On a 16,384-tip tree the per-tip solve took 5.4–7.6 s and allocated 30 GB; the selected inverse takes 0.01 s and about 1 kB per tip (0.21 s at 262,144 tips).
