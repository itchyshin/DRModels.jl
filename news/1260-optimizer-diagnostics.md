- **`bridge_diagnostics` no longer labels every counted fit `Optim.LBFGS` (#1260).** The optimizer field now follows a recorded selected-stage method (`Optim.LBFGS` / `Optim.BFGS` / `Optim.Newton` when `control_info` stored that method). A nonnegative `niterations` count is the accepted final stage, not total work, and is not itself evidence of LBFGS: qualified BFGS keeps a BFGS label, and an unrecorded Beta Newton polish is `missing` rather than inferred. Default counted non-Beta routes remain LBFGS; `-1` counts stay missing unless a method was recorded. Fitted values, likelihoods, and solver defaults are unchanged.
