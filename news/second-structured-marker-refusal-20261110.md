- **A second structured term is refused on every non-Gaussian route, not dropped.** `_split_ranef` returns only the first structured marker, so a formula such as `y ~ x + spatial(1 | g) + relmat(1 | h)` (or `relmat + animal`, `phylo + spatial`, the same kind on two groups) was fitted with the first term alone and the second vanished without a message. Confirmed on Poisson (log-likelihood and parameter count identical to the one-term fit), NB2 (including the new coordinate spatial route), Gamma, Beta, ZeroOneBeta, Binomial, BetaBinomial and CumulativeLogit; the Student coordinate route had its own guard added earlier today. The shared entry check (`_require_one_structured_marker`, run by `_drm_entry_guard`) now raises an `ArgumentError` that names both terms and the route, and says to keep one structured term or use `Gaussian()`, whose mean fits two (`phylo(1 | species) + relmat(1 | id)`). Gaussian and LogNormal (which passes its markers to the Gaussian two-structured router on `log(y)`) are exempt and unchanged; no fit that was correct, default or tolerance changes. New test `test/test_second_structured_marker_refusal.jl`.
