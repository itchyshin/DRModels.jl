- **Convergence and covariance status no longer over-claim (lane J2; #1024, #1047, #964, #956, #968, #961, #949, #1003, #1005, #1007, #1023, #952, #978, #959).** Inner Laplace mode cap exits, EM stops, temporal restarts, raw-inverse Wald sites, non-finite Hessians on constant or badly scaled responses, the location-only `resd` profile, sentinel-ending fits and the `locscale` Nelder-Mead fallback now report failure or `converged = false` instead of success. `show`, `check_drm` and `bridge_diagnostics` use `is_converged`. `heritability` / `icc` gain a `status` field and warn on non-converged, penalised or degenerate fits. Sparse phylo-mean mean-coefficient SEs now come from the joint observed information (they were conditional GLS SEs, up to ~1.5% too small). Fits that end at a likelihood clamp (sparse Laplace, Tweedie, zero-one beta, spatial Poisson) now warn. A failing boundary diagnostic now warns instead of reading as a clean fit.
