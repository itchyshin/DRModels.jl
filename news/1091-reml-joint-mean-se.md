- **Gaussian phylogenetic-mean REML: the mean-coefficient SEs are now the joint SE, pending a decision between joint and conditional (#1073).** For `y ~ x + phylo(1 | species)` with `sigma ~ 1` and `method = :REML`, the reported mean-coefficient standard errors are the **joint** ones, `(XᵀV⁻¹X)⁻¹ + G V_vv Gᵀ`: the GLS covariance plus the uncertainty of the two variance parameters carried through β̂(v), with `G = ∂β̂/∂v` and `V_vv` from the restricted Hessian. This is what drmTMB's `sdreport` reports. Earlier versions reported the **conditional** SEs `(XᵀV⁻¹X)⁻¹`, which treat the variance parameters as known. On the `ape::carnivora` fixture (taxonomic tree, Grafen branch lengths) the slope SE was 0.02378 conditional against 0.02686 joint (ratio 0.885). Shinichi has still to choose between joint and conditional; until then DRModels reports the joint SE, for drmTMB parity. The REML objective's gradient is now in closed form rather than a finite difference. REML fits on this route therefore carry `nllgrad`, and their variance-block SEs come from differences of the exact restricted score. A collapsed variance (a log-SD at its guard) still falls back to the value-based Hessian, with a warning.
