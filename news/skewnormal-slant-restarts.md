- **SkewNormal fixed-effect fits restart on the slant.** The skew-normal likelihood can have two or three local optima in the slant `nu`, and the single default start could stop at a worse one than drmTMB 0.8.1. A fit now also runs from four fixed extra starts (slant sign reversed, the first optimum mirrored, slant intercepts +8 and -8). An extra start replaces the fit so far only if Optim reports convergence, its log-likelihood is higher by more than 1e-5, and its estimate is an interior optimum (positive definite Hessian, finite and not inflated standard errors). A restart therefore never gives a lower log-likelihood, an unconverged fit or a degenerate one, and a fit whose default start was already best is unchanged. It changes the returned fit on some data sets, always to a higher log-likelihood (`fit.scales[:q2_restarts]` records the outcome). It multiplies the run time of the fit by about 8 at n = 100 and n = 1000 and by 6 to 10 at n = 10000. It is a multi-start, not a global search. A large slant estimate with a large standard error means a flat likelihood and occurs with or without the restarts. Restarts are skipped when `DRMControl` sets `start`, `multi_start`, `maxit` or `optimizer`.
