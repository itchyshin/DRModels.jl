- **SkewNormal fixed-effect fits restart on the slant.** The skew-normal likelihood can have two or three local optima in the slant `nu`, and the single default start could stop at a worse one than drmTMB 0.8.1. A fit now also runs from four fixed extra starts (slant sign reversed, the first optimum mirrored, slant intercepts +8 and -8) and keeps one only when its log-likelihood is higher by more than 1e-5; a converged fit is never exchanged for an unconverged one. This follows the restart rule of the Binomial and Gamma routes (`fit.scales[:q2_restarts]` records the outcome). It changes the returned fit on some data sets, always to a higher log-likelihood, and a fit whose default start was already best is unchanged. It costs about four extra optimiser runs. It is a multi-start, not a global search. Restarts are skipped when `DRMControl` sets `start`, `multi_start`, `maxit` or `optimizer`.
