- **LogNormal `(1 + x | g)` no longer throws a line-search `AssertionError`.** On data where the slope SD or the correlation sits at a boundary, the default L-BFGS line search could stop with `AssertionError: isfinite(phi_c) && isfinite(dphi_c)` or `B > A`, depending on the Julia version and CPU (found in the R-against-Julia grid: 2 of 12 datasets on Julia 1.10.12, others on 1.12.7 and 1.13.3). The fit is now retried from the start with a finite sentinel and BackTracking, then polished with the default line search, and it agrees with drmTMB 0.8.1 on logLik, fixed effects and log SDs. A fit that did not throw before is unchanged to the last bit (checked on 105 fits on Julia 1.10.12 and 107 on 1.13.3). `converged` stays honest and can be `false` on the retried fit.
