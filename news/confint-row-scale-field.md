- **`confint` rows carry a `scale` field.** Each row is now `(param, coef, estimate, lower, upper, scale)`. For a random-effect SD, `confint(fit; method = :profile)` returns the row on the log SD scale when the profile is interior and on the SD scale (`lower = 0`) when the lower limit reaches the boundary. `scale` says which: `:log_sd`, `:sd`, or `:link` for every other row. The numbers of every row are unchanged.
