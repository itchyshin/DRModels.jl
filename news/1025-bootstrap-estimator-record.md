- **`bootstrap_result` records which estimator it used, and the formula-based form can request REML, a non-default marginal or a MAP penalty (#1025).** The fit-based form already repeated a REML, `:VA`/`:AGHQ` or MAP seed fit in every replicate (#1038), but nothing on the result said so, and the formula-based form had no `method`, `marginal` or `penalty` keyword. The result now carries `estimation_method` and `marginal` (the seed fit) and `refit_estimation_method` and `refit_marginal` (the kept replicates, `:mixed` if they differ), and warns if the replicates used a different estimator. `bootstrap_result(formula, family; method = :REML)` fits the seed and every replicate by REML; `method` other than `:ML`/`:REML` throws `ArgumentError`. Existing result fields are unchanged. Tested on a Gaussian `(1 | g)` fit, B = 3: seed and replicates `:REML`, and the formula form matches the fit-based form on the same seed.
