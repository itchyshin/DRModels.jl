- **Docs: `predict` sets random effects to zero, and the default marginal approximation is named (#1092, #1070).** The `predict` and `predict_parameters` docstrings said random effects were "integrated out", and the `fitted` docstring called its value the "population/marginal mean". `predict` and `predict_parameters` return the population-level value with every random effect at zero (`g⁻¹(Xβ̂)`), the prediction for an average group, and so does `fitted` on the fixed-effect and ordinary `(1 | g)` routes of the GLM-type families (Gaussian, Poisson, NegBinomial2, Gamma, Beta, Binomial). The docstrings now say so; give the log-link marginal mean, `predict(fit, newdata) .* exp(re_sd(fit)[:g]^2 / 2)`; say that drmTMB's `predict(newdata)` also sets random effects to zero while its `fitted()` adds the random-effect modes; and list the routes and families whose stored `fitted` values differ (the Gaussian `phylo` mean route, ZeroOneBeta, CumulativeLogit, LogNormal). `test/test_predict_random_effects.jl` pins the behaviour on Poisson and Bernoulli `(1 | g)` fits. The drmTMB-parity and Getting-started pages now say that the default `marginal = :LA` uses each route's own integrator: per-group adaptive Gauss–Hermite quadrature with 5 nodes for an ordinary `(1 | g)` on most non-Gaussian means (21 for Tweedie, 41 for CumulativeLogit), a fixed 32-node grid for Gaussian `sigma ~ (1 | g)` and ZeroOneBeta, and the Laplace approximation on crossed and structured routes. They also say that the default and drmTMB log-likelihoods differ by about 0.001 to 0.24 units on the count, Beta, Gamma and Student datasets compared, with the default the closer to a 61-node reference; that `marginal = :Laplace` reproduces drmTMB (log-likelihoods to about 1e-8, estimates to about 5e-8 relative) and is for parity rather than a better default, except on the fixed-grid Gaussian `sigma` route; that log-likelihoods, likelihood-ratio tests and AIC/BIC compare only within one approximation; and which models accept `:Laplace`. Descriptions of the ordinary `(1 | g)` default as a fixed "32-node" grid, superseded by adaptive quadrature in v0.7.2, were corrected in `families.md`, `capabilities.md`, the Laplace-vs-variational guide, the Poisson/Beta/Gamma/NegBinomial2 docstrings and the `Laplace`/`AGHQ`/`MarginalMethod` docstrings; `capabilities.md` gains the missing Student `:Laplace` row; and `rosetta.md`, `model-workflow.md`, the prediction page and the temporal tutorial now describe predictions as set to zero rather than integrated over the random effects. The `ArgumentError` texts that still call the default "GHQ-32" are code and are left for a separate change.
