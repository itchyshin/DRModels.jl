- **Poisson `offset()` now works with a random intercept (#941).** `drm(bf(@formula(y ~ x + offset(log_exposure) + (1 | g))), Poisson(); data)` fits the standard rate GLMM on the default `:LA` route and under `marginal = :AGHQ` / `:Laplace`; the offset's coefficient is fixed at 1. With `marginal = :Laplace` the log-likelihood is drmTMB's: glmmTMB's Owls `calls ~ food + offset(log BroodSize) + (1 | nest)` gives −2603.2040 (drmTMB −2603.204) and the full `food * parent` model −2601.3880 (drmTMB −2601.3880). `offset()` with a structured marker, a random slope, crossed effects, `zi`/`hu` or `marginal = :VA` is still refused with an explicit error.
- **Three random-effect layouts drmTMB fits are ported, parity only (#960).** Gaussian with a correlated random-slope mean `(1 + x | g)` plus a `sigma` random intercept on the same factor (per-group 3-D Laplace; ChickWeight logLik −2313.4848, drmTMB −2313.485), and independent slopes `(1 | g) + (0 + x | g)` for `Gamma()` (ChickWeight −2306.1762, drmTMB −2306.176) and `Student()` (Laplace, matches drmTMB to 8 digits on a converged fit). Correlated `(1 + x | g)` for Gamma/Student is not added: drmTMB refuses it too.
