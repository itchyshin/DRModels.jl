- **Missing predictors error by name; prior weights are a keyword, not a MethodError (#1089, #1111, #931).** A raw Julia `missing` in a predictor is an `ArgumentError` pointing at `drm_listwise`, not a StatsModels `KeyError`. A complete `Union{Missing,T}` predictor stays numeric instead of dummy-coded. `drm(...; weights = w)` is accepted on every family: fixed-effect Gaussian, Poisson, Gamma and Beta multiply the per-row log-likelihood (integer weights match row duplication); random-effect and other-family routes refuse with issue #931. Unweighted fits report `weights(fit) === nothing`.
