- **Location-scale profile CI endpoints no longer come back as spurious `±Inf` from NaN / stalled inner solves.** Three root causes, none a model bug. (1) Objective-resolution stall: the backtracking (Armijo-only) L-BFGS profile nuisance solve stopped ~1e-5 from stationarity because trial objectives differ by less than the ~1e-12 noise floor at nll ~ 600, then reported `not_converged`/`not_stationary`; the exact gradient was correct (matches central differences). Such a stall is now retried once from the stall point with a Hager-Zhang line search under the same strict 1e-7 postcheck; accepted solves are untouched. (2) Stiff prior: at log L22 ~ -11.8 the prior precision has ~1e10 entries, one ulp of the latent mode moves the inner gradient by ~2e-9 > the strict `1e-9 (1+‖a‖)` certificate, the inner Newton cycled, and the objective/gradient became the 1e18/NaN sentinels. Profile solves (only) now accept the mode under `tol (1+‖a‖) + 4 eps ‖|P||a|‖` after the strict test has stalled (`relaxed=true`; the ordinary fit and Wald path is unchanged). (3) Range overflow: on a flat variance arm the geometric bracket expansion walked to |log L| ~ 700-1000 where `exp` overflows, so a flat unbounded profile was reported as a failed endpoint; log-Cholesky-diagonal arms are now capped at |log L| <= 300 and report `:no_crossing`. Together with owner decision 15 (a) (boundary log-Cholesky coordinates exempt from the stationarity gate), the slow `test_locscale_profile.jl` passes 9/9 and 45/45 on Julia 1.10 and 1.13 (main: 7 of 9 fail); new fast tests in `test_locscale_profile_nan.jl`.
