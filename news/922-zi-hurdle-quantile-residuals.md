- **Quantile residuals of zero-inflated and hurdle count fits now use the full mixture CDF (#922).** `residuals(fit; type = :quantile)` for a Poisson or NB2 fit with a `zi ~ …` or `hu ~ …` part used to build the randomized PIT from the count component alone, so under a correctly specified model the residuals were not N(0, 1). On simulated data (n = 2000, true model fitted) the scaled Kolmogorov–Smirnov statistic √n·D was 13.0 (ZI Poisson), 12.0 (ZI NB2), 9.3 (hurdle Poisson) and 6.2 (hurdle NB2), with residual means between −0.64 and −0.26; it is now 0.48–0.88, with means within 0.005 of 0.
  - Zero-inflated: `F(y) = π + (1 − π)·F_count(y)` for `y ≥ 0`. Hurdle: `F(0) = p0` and `F(y) = p0 + (1 − p0)·F_trunc(y)` for `y ≥ 1`, where `F_trunc` is the zero-truncated count CDF. Each observation is randomized within `[F(y − 1), F(y)]`, as for the other discrete families.
  - The hurdle part reuses the log-space zero-truncated Poisson and NB2 CDFs of `TruncatedPoisson()` and `TruncatedNegBinomial2()`, so a hurdle NB2 fit at extreme dispersion cannot return `NaN`. Those two families' residuals are unchanged.
  - drmTMB's `residuals(fit, type = "quantile")` already uses the mixture CDF for `zi_poisson`, `zi_nbinom2` and `hurdle_nbinom2` (drmTMB has no hurdle Poisson). On one shared dataset (n = 2000, drmTMB origin/main 0eb046785) the PIT interval bounds `[F(y − 1), F(y)]` agree to 1.7e-15 when DRModels.jl evaluates them at drmTMB's fitted parameters, and to 3.6e-12 at each package's own fit; with drmTMB's uniforms the residuals agree to 2.1e-11.
