- **Binomial independent random effects with a covariate in the SD formulas (`sd(g, coef(.)) ~ z`) now restart the optimiser.** From its single default start L-BFGS could stop at a smooth, converged interior point 1 to 6 nats below a point the same objective scores higher (the 2 wave-8 cells `binomial|indep|q2|sd-z` and `q3|sd-z`: -255.2241 and -254.5596 before, -253.1450 and -253.1280 now; drmTMB 0.8.1 reports -253.5291, but the two programs score the same estimate 0.29 apart, so this is not a same-objective comparison). Such fits now run the shared `_q2_boundary_restarts` unconditionally (an alternative start is kept only on a clear objective gain, so a fit whose first optimum stands is unchanged; an alternative that does not converge replaces a converged first fit only if it gains more than 1 nat, so a restart does not turn a converged fit into an unconverged one) and record the outcome in `fit.scales[:q2_restarts]`. Intercept-only SD formulas and correlated blocks are untouched. Cost on a 400-row fit (medians over 40 generated seeds, Julia 1.10, 20 processes sharing one host): about 0.9 s instead of 0.1 s for two random terms, about 7.5 s instead of 1 s for three; the worst fits took about 1.75 s and 12.6 s. Test: `test/test_binomial_indep_sdz_restarts.jl`; evidence: `docs/dev-log/2026-11-09-binomial-indep-restarts.md`. Convergence caveat: the restarts raise the log-likelihood and, on most seeds, the converged count, but a fit can still end unconverged (on the Totoro measurement, 3 of 40 generated seeds for three random terms, and 1 of 40 for two terms before the acceptance rule was tightened), and the restarts cost about 8 times the time of a single fit. For four random terms an independent check on 20 seeds (not committed) found the restarts never worsened a fit, raised the converged count from 10 to 19, and took a median 174 s per fit against 22 s.
