Fix: Gaussian fits with a response on a tiny scale (residual SD about 1e-4) are no longer flagged unconverged at the optimum. Every route that already used the unit-free Newton-step check (Gaussian ML and REML fixed effects, Gaussian random effects, Gaussian `sd ~` models, Student) now sets `converged` from that check without also requiring `Optim.converged`, which fails a line-search stall at the optimum. The unit-free check also judges a singular Hessian on its correlation form, so the verdict no longer moves with the units of y. Stopping on the iteration cap still fails the flag (#983).
