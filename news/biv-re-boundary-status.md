- `convergence_status()` now reads the group SDs of the bivariate `(1 | p | g)` routes. The 2 x 2 group covariance of these fits is kept outside the blocks that `re_sd()` reads, so a group SD that had collapsed to zero was not seen by the boundary rule (SD below 1e-4, as in drmTMB). The two group SDs now count, and such a fit is `:boundary` (or `:degenerate` when the covariance or standard-error rules fire first), so `is_converged()` is false for it. A group correlation of plus or minus one has no rule of its own, as in drmTMB; it is caught only through the covariance and standard-error rules.
