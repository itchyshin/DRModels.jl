- **A `Student()` fit whose `ν` runs off to the Gaussian limit is no longer reported as converged, and the `(1 | g)` and `(1 + x | g)` routes try a second start** (Refs #937). On `Reaction ~ Days + (1 | Subject)`, `sigma ~ Days` (sleepstudy) the one LBFGS run slid down the flat `ν` ridge to `ν ≈ 1.3e12` (logLik −891.912, the Gaussian fit) while the heavy-tailed optimum is −887.513 at `ν ≈ 4.6`. `(1 | g)` and `(1 + x | g)` on the mean now restart once from `ν = 4` with the random-effect scale at half the residual SD (as the crossed-intercept route restarts) whenever the first run stops with `ν` above 1000 or without the gradient criterion, and keep the better minimiser. The opt-in `marginal = :Laplace` route and the fixed-effects-only `Student()` route get no second start yet (#937 stays open for them; the sleepstudy `:Laplace` fit still stops at the Gaussian limit but is no longer reported as converged). `convergence_status` / `is_converged` also reject, for any `Student()` fit, a fitted `ν` above 1000 in any row (`:boundary`) and an objective gradient above `1e-6` per observation (`:degenerate`). This is a DRModels.jl addition to the shared status rules; drmTMB has no counterpart yet.
