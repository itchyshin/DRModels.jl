- The bivariate `(1 | p | g)` route with `method = :REML` now refuses by name when the fixed-effect design of the observed rows of a response is rank-deficient, for example a covariate that is constant (or equal to another covariate) on the rows where that response is observed but varies in the full data. Before, the fit stopped with a raw `SingularException: matrix is singular; factorization failed`. The message names the response, the rank and the columns. `method = :ML` is unchanged. REML together with `miss_control(response = "include")` on this route goes beyond drmTMB 0.8.1, which refuses that combination. The docs state the missing-at-random assumption and that a REML log-likelihood is not comparable between fits with different fixed effects or different missing patterns.
