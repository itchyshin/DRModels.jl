- **Gaussian crossed random effects `(1 | g) + (1 | h)` no longer scale as n²** (#977). The multi-component closed-form route took a dense QR of the (n + q) × q stacked design on every objective and gradient call; it now uses a sparse QR (the sparse Cholesky factor of the Woodbury capacitance, formed without squaring the condition number, so the #835/#837 cancellation guard is kept) and a Takahashi selected inverse for the analytic gradient. Estimates, logLik and BLUPs are unchanged (identity to ≤ 1e-8, pinned in `test/test_multi_re_sparse_kernel.jl`). For q above 200 random-effect columns the Wald vcov is a central difference of the exact analytic gradient instead of ForwardDiff through the dense objective. Timings: `bench/bench_crossed_sparse_977.jl`.
