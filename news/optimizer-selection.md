- `DRMControl(optimizer = :lbfgs)` and `DRMControl(optimizer = :bfgs)` now select Optim.jl's named method directly on the fixed-effect univariate ML routes and Gaussian ML with one mean random intercept. `:default` keeps each route's previous optimizer and restart policy. `control_info(fit)` records the requested and actual method, every attempted start, the selected stage and native stopping diagnostics. Unqualified latent, bivariate and REML routes reject an explicit choice; profile/bootstrap refits do not inherit it. No convergence or inference advantage is implied by selecting a method.
