- **`residuals(fit; type = :quantile)` on the random-effect SD-regression routes now conditions on the Laplace modes, as drmTMB's does; `Ainv` works on Gamma and Gaussian animal q2.** The ordinary q2 / q3 / q4, relmat / animal / phylo / spatial q2, labelled `(1 + x | p | g)`, Gamma / NB2 two-field and joint mean + `sigma` routes were judged with the random effects set to 0 (SD of the residuals 1.07-3.27 on the test fixtures at the population level, against 0.80-0.99 with the modes, as measured); each route now records the per-row mode contribution at `theta_hat` (`scales[:cond_eta_re]`, also exported by the R bridge) and the residual uses it, so the SD is 0.80-0.99. `fitted(fit)` is unchanged (population level). Checked in this repository against an independent dense computation of the modes and the residuals (agreement 1e-8 or better). The comparison with native drmTMB 0.8.1 (labelled animal q2 for Poisson, NB2, Gamma and Gaussian; ordinary q2 for Poisson and NB2; conditional mean, PIT bounds and deterministic residuals) was run on the R side and its numbers are not stored here; the evidence is in itchyshin/drmTMB PR 1684, folders wave3-labelled-phylo-spatial, wave3-labelled-ordinary, wave4-postfit and wave4-reml. Only the dense-oracle agreement is reproducible from this repository. `drm(...; Ainv = …)` on `animal(1 + x | g)` and `animal(1 + x | p | g)` with SD formulas was a `MethodError` for Gamma and Gaussian although drmTMB accepts it and Poisson / NB2 already did; it now fits exactly the model `A = inv(Ainv)` gives. The precision-input forms `relmat(…, Q = Q)` and the `simulate`/`bootstrap_result` keyword `Ainv` for Gamma / Gaussian are not added.
