- **A fit that ends at the log-SD search bound is now reported as a boundary fit.** On the independent-term Laplace route (`(1 | g) + (0 + x | g)`, non-Gaussian families) the objective rejects a log SD below -30 or above 30; that value is unchanged. When the best fit runs to the lower end, `convergence_status(fit)` now returns `:boundary` (it returned `:degenerate`), and the fit warns once, pointing to `lrt_boundary` (50:50 chi-bar-square mixture) to test the variance against zero with a reduced fit that drops the term. A profile interval may not converge on such a fit. The estimates, log-likelihood and `fit.converged` are unchanged. drmTMB follows the same ridge further out, so its log-likelihood can be higher. `sigma(fit)` is unchanged by the flag (the marker is not returned).
