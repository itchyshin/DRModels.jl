- **The bridge payload exports the conditional structured field for relmat, animal and spatial mean fields (batch 34).** A new `conditional_structured` payload key carries `eta_re`, the per-row link-scale contribution of the field at its inner Laplace mode (data row order), and `modes`, the mode of each field component in first-seen group order (the row order of the `K` / `A` / `Q` matrix). drmTMB rebuilds `fitted()` and `residuals()` from it, so they are now the conditional mean as in native drmTMB instead of the population-level value. Fitters changed: the Gaussian structured SD route (`report_sd`, ML; the exported mode is `b = u / sd`, the same latent scale as the other families), the Poisson, NB2 and Gamma structured SD Laplace fitters (relmat, animal and coordinate spatial, intercept and slope; Poisson `Ainv`), the Binomial related-matrix SD Laplace fitter (relmat, animal `A` or `Ainv`, spatial; logit, probit, cloglog) and the Binomial animal q2 fitter (two modes, intercept and slope). The mode is a fresh inner solve at the converged `theta`, not the last finite-difference stencil point; in the NB2 fitter it is taken before the objective is stored because `laplace` writes the boxed `value` shared with that function (the first version of this change, with the call just before `DrmFit(...)`, moved the NB2 log-likelihood by 28). `DRModels.structured_conditional(fit)` returns `(eta_re, modes)`. Not exported, so unchanged: phylogenetic fields, Binomial fields combined with an ordinary random effect, mesh and crossed routes, the Gamma animal intercept-plus-slope route. DRModels.jl's own `fitted(fit)` and `residuals(fit)` stay population level. New `test/test_structured_conditional_export.jl` checks `modes` and `eta_re` against a hand-built dense Newton oracle (hand-derived per-family derivatives, no fitter code) on shuffled-row fixtures for Gaussian, Poisson, NB2, Gamma and Binomial, intercept and slope, at 1e-6 fixed beforehand, and that the stored objective equals `nll(theta_hat)` to 1e-4. No calibration or broad parity claim.
