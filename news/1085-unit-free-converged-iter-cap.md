- **Near-zero-variance Gaussian fits report `converged` at the optimum (#983, #1085).** `_fit_fixed_gaussian` accepts a unit-free Newton decrement as stationarity even when LBFGS hits its iteration cap on noise-limited steps (the absolute `g_tol` cannot be met when the mean gradient scales like `1/σ²`). The decrement is computed after diagonal equilibration so an ill-conditioned but PD Hessian (mean block `~1/σ²`, log-σ block `O(n)`) does not fail the Cholesky. A plateau with a large decrement (#944) and a run-off scale (#1059) stay `false`.
