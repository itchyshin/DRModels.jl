- **One `control = DRMControl(...)` keyword on `drm()`, `gradient(fit)` / `hessian(fit)`, and real prior `weights` (lane N3; closes #942, #948, #994, #971; advances #931).** `DRMControl(maxit, start, multi_start, se)` uses drmTMB's `drm_control()` names and goes through all 19 `drm` methods. `se = false` now works on every route, including `Gaussian()` and `LogNormal()`, which had no `se` keyword (#942); the fixed-effect routes skip the Hessian, and any other route drops the standard errors after the fit. `maxit`, `start` and `multi_start` work on the fixed-effect routes of the univariate families, including the main Gaussian location-scale route (#948, #994); a model with random or structured terms refuses them by name instead of ignoring them. `control_info(fit)` reports the log-likelihood of each start, the kept start, whether the starts agree, and what `se = false` did. `gradient(fit)` and `hessian(fit)` return the gradient and Hessian of the negative log-likelihood the optimiser minimised, in `coef(fit)` order and on `coef(fit)`'s scale (#971). **Behaviour change (#931):** `weights(fit)` no longer returns a misleading `ones(nobs(fit))`; it returns the prior weights, or `nothing` when none were given. `drm(...; weights = w)` multiplies each row's log-likelihood (drmTMB semantics) on the fixed-effect ML routes of the univariate families; every other route (random or structured effects, bivariate, REML) refuses `weights =` with a named error.
