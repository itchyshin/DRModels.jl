- **Gaussian (and LogNormal) quantile residuals with a lone mean random intercept now return the exact z-score (#1103).** The marginal is `N(μ, σ² + σ_b²)` (or the log-y form for LogNormal). The previous `Φ⁻¹(Φ(z))` round trip lost upper-tail precision and capped `|r|` at about 8.13. At `σ_b/σ = 10` the round-trip error over `|z| ≤ 4` was about 0.12; the residual now matches `z` to machine precision. Other families still use the PIT plus `Φ⁻¹`.
