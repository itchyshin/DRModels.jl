- **Prior `weights` on four more routes (drmTMB parity).** `drm(...; weights = w)` now fits, with the weights as row log-likelihood multipliers as in drmTMB, on: Gaussian with one ordinary `(1 | g)` (ML and `method = :REML`), Poisson with one `(1 | g)` (default marginal and `marginal = :Laplace`; not `:AGHQ`, `:VA` or REML), Gaussian `meta_V(v)`, and the residual bivariate Gaussian (not REML, and not together with `V`, as in drmTMB). Zero weights and non-integer weights are accepted (an integer weight equals that many replicated rows, which the tests use as an oracle). Every other random-effect, structured, `sd()` or `control` shape keeps its refusal.
