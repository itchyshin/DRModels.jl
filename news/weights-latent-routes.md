- **Prior `weights` on four more routes (drmTMB parity).** `drm(...; weights = w)` now fits, with the weights as row log-likelihood multipliers as in drmTMB, on: Gaussian with one ordinary `(1 | g)` (ML and `method = :REML`), Poisson with one `(1 | g)` (default marginal and `marginal = :Laplace`; not `:AGHQ`, `:VA` or REML), Gaussian `meta_V(v)` (ML only), and the residual bivariate Gaussian (not REML, and not together with `V`, as in drmTMB). Zero weights and non-integer weights are accepted (an integer weight equals that many replicated rows, which the tests use as an oracle). Every other random-effect, structured, `sd()` or `control` shape keeps its refusal. `weights` together with `meta_V` and `method = :REML` is refused by name.
- **Weighted Poisson `(1 | g)` matches drmTMB only with `marginal = :Laplace`.** With `marginal = :Laplace` the log-likelihood and estimates agree with drmTMB 0.8.1 inside the usual bounds. With the default marginal the log-likelihood differs from drmTMB by 0.0025 to 0.028 (and a fixed-effect standard error by up to 0.3%), with or without weights. The cause is the integration: drmTMB uses a Laplace approximation, while the default here integrates the random intercept by adaptive quadrature. The weights are not the cause.
- **`update()` on a weighted fit now needs `weights =` stated.** Before, `update(fit, ...)` on any weighted fit (including the fixed-effect routes that already accepted weights) dropped the weights without a message. Now it throws an `ArgumentError` unless you pass `weights = weights(fit)` (or the original full-length vector if rows with a missing response were dropped), new weights, or `weights = nothing` to drop them on purpose. The bivariate bootstrap now keeps the weights in its refits (it used to refit unweighted).
