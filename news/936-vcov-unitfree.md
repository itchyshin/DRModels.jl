- **The covariance guard's singular / boundary decision no longer depends on units (#936).** `_vcov_from_hessian` compared the smallest and largest eigenvalues of the *unscaled* Hessian, so an uncentred `year` (ratio 4.4e-12) or `y * 1e4` (5.4e-9) made a well-identified fit warn "at a variance/dispersion boundary", and at `y * 1e8` the pseudo-inverse truncated the mean directions (intercept SE 13,088 instead of 9.6e6). The test now runs on the equilibrated Hessian: regression coefficients are scaled by their own curvature, and the coordinates each fitter declares unit-free (log-SDs, log-dispersions, atanh-correlations, cut-points, the 0/1 columns of dispersion-type formulas) are scaled in natural units, so a flat one is still reported as a boundary. Healthy Hessians are inverted exactly as before; the fallback pseudo-inverse is now taken on the equilibrated matrix, so it no longer truncates real directions because of units. The singular warning now says whether a flat unit-free coordinate or a non-identified combination of parameters caused it, and reports `null_direction_coordinates`. On the #936 data the standard errors match the closed-form Gaussian ML values to 1e-8 relative at every scaling, and match drmTMB 0.7.1 to 3.3e-7 where drmTMB converges.
