- **The covariance guard's singular / boundary decision no longer depends on units (#936).** `_vcov_from_hessian` compared the smallest and largest eigenvalues of the *unscaled* Hessian, so an uncentred `year` (ratio 4.4e-12) or `y * 1e4` (5.4e-9) made a well-identified fit warn "at a variance/dispersion boundary", and at `y * 1e8` the pseudo-inverse truncated real directions (intercept SE 13,088 instead of 9.6e6). The guard now asks two separate questions. A coordinate each fitter declares unit-free (log-SDs, log-dispersions, atanh-correlations, cut-points, and the 0/1 columns of dispersion formulas and of log/logit-link location formulas) is at a boundary when its curvature is within 1e-4 of zero in natural units (a Wald SE of 100 or more on the log scale). The remaining coordinates, each rescaled by its own curvature, are non-identified when their smallest eigenvalue is at most 3e-8 of the largest. Healthy Hessians are inverted exactly as before. The fallback pseudo-inverse is taken on the rescaled matrix, so it no longer truncates real directions. The warning now says which of the two causes fired and reports `null_direction_coordinates`. On the #936 data the standard errors match the closed-form Gaussian ML values to 1e-8 relative at every scaling, and drmTMB 0.7.1 to 3.3e-7 where drmTMB converges.
