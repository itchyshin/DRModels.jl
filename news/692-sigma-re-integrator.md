- **Docs: the random-dispersion tutorial now says when the default integrator and Laplace differ (#692).** For `sigma ~ (1 | g)` the default `marginal = :LA` is non-adaptive 32-node Gauss–Hermite quadrature, close to exact for small groups and less accurate for groups of hundreds of rows; `marginal = :Laplace` matches drmTMB and suits large groups. The tutorial also says the `sigma` intercept is E[log σ], not log E[σ]; they differ by τ²/2.
