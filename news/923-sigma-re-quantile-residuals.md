- **Fix: quantile residuals of a Gaussian `sigma ~ 1 + (1 | g)` fit no longer treat the scale random intercept as a mean intercept (#923).** `residuals(fit; type = :quantile)` sent every fit with exactly one random-intercept SD to the σ_b-marginal route (#760), whatever axis the intercept was on. A scale-only fit therefore integrated the MEAN over N(0, τ̂²), with τ̂ the log-σ random-effect SD, which is a different model: on the new test's simulated fit (τ = 0.6, 30 groups of 20) the residual SD was 0.85 instead of about 1, and residuals sat up to 1.22 from the correct reference.
  - A lone log-σ intercept is now integrated out of the scale, σ_i e^b with b ~ N(0, τ̂²), on the same 32 Gauss–Hermite nodes. On that fit the residuals match an adaptive-quadrature reference to 1.4e-6 and their SD is 1.01.
  - The routing keys on the `<g>_logsigma` name of the random-effect SD. A lone mean intercept (`(1 | g)`, `phylo(1 | g)`, `relmat(1 | g)`) keeps the σ_b-marginal route, and a mean plus sigma `(1 | g)` pair keeps both random effects at 0. (`drm()` fits a sigma random intercept for Gaussian only; a non-Gaussian one is refused before residuals are reached.)
  - drmTMB's quantile residuals for these fits condition on the fitted modes instead: its `predict()` adds them, and its residuals equal `(y − μ̂)/σ̂` with the log-σ mode included to 4e-15. DRModels.jl marginalises the random effect instead, the population-level reference it already uses for the mean intercept, so per-row values differ from drmTMB's by design, and how closely they track drmTMB's varies from one simulated replicate to the next.
  - What to expect: residuals of shapes judged with the random effects at 0 (crossed, correlated slopes, a mean and a sigma intercept together) are over-dispersed under a correct model, so a bent QQ plot is not by itself misfit. Gaussian `sigma ~ 1 + (1 | g)` residuals can be under-dispersed when the log-σ SD is moderate or large, because the default `marginal = :LA` fit integrates that intercept on a fixed prior-scale grid; refit with `marginal = :AGHQ` to check.
