- **Fix: quantile residuals of a Gaussian `sigma ~ 1 + (1 | g)` fit no longer treat the scale random intercept as a mean intercept (#923).** `residuals(fit; type = :quantile)` sent every fit with exactly one random-intercept SD to the σ_b-marginal route (#760), whatever axis the intercept was on. A scale-only fit therefore integrated the MEAN over N(0, τ̂²), with τ̂ the log-σ random-effect SD, which is a different model: on a simulated fit with τ = 0.6 the residual SD was 0.75 instead of about 1.
  - A lone log-σ intercept is now integrated out of the scale, σ_i e^b with b ~ N(0, τ̂²), on the same 32 Gauss–Hermite nodes. The residuals match an adaptive-quadrature reference to 1e-6, and their SD is close to 1 under a correct model.
  - The routing keys on the `<g>_logsigma` name of the random-effect SD. A lone mean intercept (`(1 | g)`, `phylo(1 | g)`, `relmat(1 | g)`) keeps the σ_b-marginal route, and a mean plus sigma `(1 | g)` pair keeps both random effects at 0. A non-Gaussian σ-axis intercept also keeps b = 0, because no σ-marginal mapping is verified for those families.
  - drmTMB's quantile residuals for these fits condition on the fitted modes instead: its `predict()` adds them, and its residuals equal `(y − μ̂)/σ̂` with the log-σ mode included to 4e-15. DRModels.jl keeps the population-level reference it already uses for the mean intercept, so per-row values differ from drmTMB's.
