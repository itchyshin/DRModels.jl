- **AR1 and OU `temporal()` fits: standardised residuals are now conditional on the fitted modes, as in drmTMB.** `residuals(fit; type = :quantile)` used to divide `y − Xβ̂` by σ̂ alone. That ignored the temporal and `(1 | id)` covariance, so it was not the residual of either package's model. It now returns `(y − Xβ̂ − b̂ − ŝ)/σ̂`, where `ŝ` is the temporal mode and `b̂` is the `(1 | id)` mode (or the phylogenetic stable mode of a paired `phylo()` + OU fit). This is drmTMB's Pearson and quantile residual for these routes (drmTMB `07d1612ea`; for the paired route, `012258e9f`): its `fitted()` adds every mode.
  - **Meaning.** The residual is the estimated noise `E[ε | y]/σ̂`, so its variance is below 1 even when the model is true. It is not whitened against the marginal covariance. Homogeneous Toeplitz fits keep their whitened residual, because they have no modes.
  - **Caveats.** At a residual-SD boundary (`sigma_ratio` in `check_drm(fit).temporal_boundary`) the temporal path absorbs the data: as σ̂ goes to 0 these residuals collapse toward 0 and cannot reveal outliers, so check the boundary first. The temporal routes do not clamp the far tail; the PIT routes of every other fit cap `|r|` at 8.126.
  - **Unchanged.** `residuals(fit)` (`type = :response`) stays `y − Xβ̂`, matching `fitted`. drmTMB's response residual subtracts its conditional `fitted()`. The bridge payload's `fitted` / `residuals` stay population level too; its comment no longer claims they equal drmTMB's for random-effect, phylogenetic or temporal fits.
  - **Checks.** A dense test checks the residual against `σ̂ V⁻¹(y − Xβ̂)` for AR1 and OU, with and without `(1 | id)`, and for the paired fit. The parity test also checks it against the same dense formula at drmTMB's stored estimates, to 1e-8 (measured at most 2.1e-9).
  - **Parity.** Every temporal parity cell now stores drmTMB's Pearson residuals. The eight AR1 / OU cells were regenerated at drmTMB `07d1612ea`, the head of open drmTMB PR #1447 as of 2026-10-02. The two paired `phylo()` + OU cells gained the block from drmTMB `012258e9f`, the head of open draft PR #1448 as of 2026-10-02; every other number in them is unchanged. DRModels.jl matches the residuals to 6.9e-7 (vignette-ou), 2.9e-7 (vignette-ar1-ri) and at most 2.1e-9 on the other eleven cells (Julia 1.10.12 and 1.13). The two larger gaps are drmTMB's: it builds its modes from TMB's `last.par.best`, which for those two fits are not the modes at its reported optimum, so its stored residual misses the exact `σ̂ V⁻¹(y − Xβ̂)` at its own estimates by exactly those amounts, while DRModels.jl's residual is exact. The OU cells are bit-identical to the earlier `dc81bb368` numbers. The AR1 cells moved by at most 4.4e-13 relative on the estimates, and both vignette-ar1-ri treatment profile endpoints moved by at most 3.5e-10 absolute (5.5e-10 relative).
  - **Docs.** The `residuals` docstring now says what each route returns and what a correct model implies: approximately standard normal with no random effect, marginal over σ̂_b for one `(1 | g)` on the mean, conditional on the modes for AR1 / OU, and whitened for homogeneous Toeplitz (where drmTMB's own `type = "quantile"` is not whitened, so compare with its Pearson residuals). It also lists TruncatedPoisson and names SkewNormal, with Tweedie, as unsupported. The post-fit page no longer says quantile residuals cover only Gaussian and Poisson, or that a well-specified model always gives ≈ N(0, 1).
