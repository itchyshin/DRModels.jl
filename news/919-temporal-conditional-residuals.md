- **AR1 and OU `temporal()` fits: standardised residuals are now conditional on the fitted modes, as in drmTMB.** `residuals(fit; type = :quantile)` used to divide `y − Xβ̂` by σ̂ alone. That ignored the temporal and `(1 | id)` covariance, so it was not the residual of either package's model. It now returns `(y − Xβ̂ − b̂ − ŝ)/σ̂`, where `ŝ` is the temporal mode and `b̂` is the `(1 | id)` mode (or the phylogenetic stable mode of a paired `phylo()` + OU fit). This is drmTMB's Pearson and quantile residual for these routes: its `fitted()` adds every mode.
  - **Meaning.** The residual is the estimated noise `E[ε | y]/σ̂`, so its variance is below 1 even when the model is true. It is not whitened against the marginal covariance. Homogeneous Toeplitz fits keep their whitened residual, because they have no modes.
  - **Unchanged.** `residuals(fit)` (`type = :response`) stays `y − Xβ̂`, matching `fitted`. drmTMB's response residual subtracts its conditional `fitted()`.
  - **Checks.** A dense test checks the residual against `σ̂ V⁻¹(y − Xβ̂)` for AR1 and OU, with and without `(1 | id)`, and for the paired fit.
  - **Parity.** Every wave-1 parity cell now stores drmTMB's Pearson residuals; DRModels.jl matches them to at most 6.9e-7 (Julia 1.10.12 and 1.13). The cells were regenerated at drmTMB `07d1612ea`, the final head of drmTMB PR #1447. The OU cells are bit-identical to the earlier `dc81bb368` numbers. The AR1 cells moved by at most 4.4e-13 relative on the estimates and 3.5e-10 on one profile endpoint.
