- **`residuals(fit; type = :quantile, quantile_reference = :plugin)` gives drmTMB's plug-in quantile residual (#1101).** The default `quantile_reference = :marginal` is unchanged: a mean random effect is averaged out of the reference distribution. `:plugin` evaluates the family CDF at the fitted-row parameters with the random-effect modes plugged in (the mean of `fitted(fit)`), as drmTMB's `residuals(type = "quantile")` does. On a Gaussian `y ~ x + (1 | g)` fit it equals drmTMB's residual to 2e-11. It throws where `fitted(fit)` has no modes, for a random effect on `sigma`, and for `ZeroOneBeta` / `CumulativeLogit` with a random effect. Without a random effect on the mean both settings agree.
