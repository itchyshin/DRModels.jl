- **REML for Gaussian structured intercept-and-slope SD-regression routes.** `method = :REML` now fits `KIND(1 + x | id)` with `sd(id, LEVEL, coef(intercept)) ~ z0` and `sd(id, LEVEL, coef(x)) ~ z1` for the shapes native drmTMB 0.8.1 accepts under REML: `phylo` labelled or unlabelled, and unlabelled `animal`, `relmat` and `spatial` with `sigma ~ 1`. The ordinary route, the joint mean + `sigma` route, labelled non-phylo blocks and structured `sigma` effects are still refused. `reml_loglik(fit)` matches a dense restricted log-likelihood written without package code (tolerance 1e-6, `test/test_gaussian_structured_q2_reml.jl`). The comparison with native drmTMB is on the R side (itchyshin/drmTMB PR 1684, folder `wave4-reml`); no R numbers are stored in this repository.
