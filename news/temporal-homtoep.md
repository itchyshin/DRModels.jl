- **Homogeneous Toeplitz temporal covariance on the Gaussian mean: `temporal(1 | id, occ, homtoep)` (D-311; twin of drmTMB's `temporal(1 | id, time = occ, structure = "homtoep")`).** On a complete panel (every `id` observed at the same 3–12 equally spaced integer occasions) each series' responses have covariance σ²R with R a free positive-definite Toeplitz correlation matrix: one correlation per lag (`temporal_parameters(fit).cor`, drmTMB's `cor_lag1…`), estimated through partial autocorrelations (`coef(fit, :temporal_pac)`, atanh scale) so every fitted R is positive definite. As in drmTMB, `sigma` is the TOTAL within-series SD and there is no separate process SD, residual SD or `(1 | id)` — they are not identified when every lag is free — and there are no latent states (`ranef` is empty, `fitted` is Xβ). The exact likelihood uses the Durbin–Levinson innovations factorisation (O(K²) per series, `log v` formed without the `1 − tanh²` cancellation); it matches a BigFloat dense oracle to ≤ 2e-15 relative with partial autocorrelations up to ±0.995, and the parameterisation is checked against dense Schur complements. `simulate`/`bootstrap_ci` draw each series from σ²R. drmTMB's panel rules are refused by name (incomplete or unequally spaced panels, fewer than 3 or more than 12 occasions, fractional time, an ordinary `(1 | id)`). Two drmTMB parity cells (drmTMB development branch `ceff79d`): logLik ≤ 1e-12, β and σ ≤ 2e-11 relative, lag correlations ≤ 5e-10.
