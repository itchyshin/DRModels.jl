- **`predict(newdata)` with a non-finite predictor is an error.** A `NaN` or `Inf` predictor in a prediction table used to come back as a `NaN` fitted value. It now raises `ArgumentError` naming the predictor and the row. A `NaN` predictor on a row whose *response* is `missing` or `NaN` is still dropped with that row at fit time.

- **Non-finite inputs are rejected, and a non-finite fit is no longer `converged` (#1009, #1012, #1019, #1021).** `Inf` or `-Inf` in a response, and any non-finite predictor, offset, weight or coordinate, now raises `ArgumentError` naming that argument and the first offending row. `missing` and `NaN` in a response are still omitted as unobserved rows. The same check sits on every formula route (Gaussian fixed effects, mixed `(1 | g)`, spatial, temporal, phylo, bivariate, cross-family, Student, the formula bridge) plus `fit_mixed_family`, the q2 bridge entries and `associate_pairs`. A margin fitted after dropping missing rows can no longer be frozen into `associate_pairs` (that used to return `eta ≈ 0.995` with log-likelihood `-floatmax`). `DrmFit` never stores `converged = true` when the log-likelihood is non-finite or a failed-fit sentinel (`-1e18`, `-floatmax`, or any value `≤ -1e15`) or when any coefficient is non-finite; `is_converged` agrees.
