- **Phylogenetic stable intercept + OU temporal deviations on the Gaussian mean: `phylo(1 | species) + temporal(1 | species, elapsed, ou)` with `drm(...; tree)` (D-311; twin of drmTMB's paired provider `phylo(1 | species, tree = tree) + temporal(1 | species, time = elapsed, structure = "ou")`).** Closely related species share a stable baseline (`a ~ N(0, σ_a² C)`, `C` the tree's tip correlation matrix, as drmTMB) while each species makes its own OU departures through time; the two are additive, not a separable phylogeny-by-time field. The exact marginal is evaluated in O(n): wave 1's per-series Kalman filter reduces each species to a Gaussian factor in its stable effect, and the tree is integrated by an exact pruning (upward message) pass, equivalent to the zero-fill sparse Cholesky of the joint tree + OU precision and generic in the number type, so ForwardDiff runs through it. It matches a dense `MvNormal` oracle to ≤ 1e-11 at random parameters on ultrametric and non-ultrametric trees, including decay → 0 / ∞ and σ_a → 0 / large. `temporal_parameters(fit).sd_phylo` (drmTMB `sd_phylo_stable`), `ranef(fit)[:species_phylo]` (stable modes) and `ranef(fit)[:species]` (OU modes); `simulate`/`bootstrap_ci` draw a fresh phylogenetic vector, fresh OU paths and noise. drmTMB's pairing rules are mirrored and refused by name (OU only, unlabelled intercept on the same grouping, no ordinary `(1 | species)`, ≥ 3 species each with ≥ 2 times, ≥ 3 distinct lags, tree tips exactly the observed species). Two drmTMB parity cells (drmTMB draft PR #1448, final head `012258e9f`), a 24-species fixture and drmTMB's 60-species article data: logLik to ≤ 5.4e-12, every estimate to ≤ 1.6e-11 relative, conditional fitted values to ≤ 1.7e-12. The temporal tutorial gains a phylogenetic section. No interval claim: drmTMB's article marks this model development-only.
- **Temporal boundary diagnostic (twin of drmTMB's `temporal_boundary` check, drmTMB #1447 / #1448).** Every `temporal()` fit (AR1, OU, paired phylo + OU) is checked against drmTMB's rules: residual σ̂ below 1e-3 · sd(y), OU decay × longest series span below 1e-4 (correlation ≈ 1 at every lag) or × shortest gap above 30 (≈ 0 at every lag), and AR1 |φ̂| above 0.999. A fit that meets any of them warns at fit time (quiet inside bootstrap and profile refits) and reports `check_drm(fit).temporal_boundary = (at_boundary = true, findings = […])`; drmTMB calls this `convergence_status() == "boundary"`. On drmTMB's own boundary panel, DRModels.jl reaches decay ≈ 1e-18 with the same β, σ and process SD. Its logLik, −45.5124249, equals a 256-bit dense evaluation; drmTMB's printed −45.5124225 is 2.5e-6 off its own exact value as decay → 0. Profile `confint` on a paired phylo + OU fit warns that the intervals are not calibrated, as drmTMB does, and a tree with unobserved tips is refused with those tips named.
