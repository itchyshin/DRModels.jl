- **Phylogenetic stable intercept + OU temporal deviations on the Gaussian mean: `phylo(1 | species) + temporal(1 | species, elapsed, ou)` with `drm(...; tree)` (D-311; twin of drmTMB's paired provider `phylo(1 | species, tree = tree) + temporal(1 | species, time = elapsed, structure = "ou")`, drmTMB draft #1448).** Closely related species share a stable baseline (`a ~ N(0, σ_a² C)`, `C` the tree's tip correlation matrix, as drmTMB), while each species makes its own OU departures through time. The two are additive; this is not a separable phylogeny-by-time field.
  - **Engine.** The exact marginal costs O(n). Wave 1's per-series Kalman filter reduces each species to a Gaussian factor in its stable effect, and an exact pruning (upward-message) pass integrates the tree. That pass is the zero-fill sparse Cholesky of the joint precision, and ForwardDiff runs through it.
  - **Trees, as drmTMB.** The tree must be ultrametric (drmTMB's relative `sqrt(eps)` tolerance). Zero-length branches are accepted: the route reads the Newick without the sparse-precision assembly, and the shared parser is unchanged.
  - **Checks** (Julia 1.10.12):
    - Dense `MvNormal` oracle: |Δnll| ≤ 9.2e-13 at random parameters, decay → 0 / ∞ and σ_a → 0 / large.
    - Pruning pass on non-ultrametric trees (internal identities, not a user route): exact to 1e-10.
    - Zero-length internal and tip branches: dense oracle to 1e-10.
  - **Accessors.** `temporal_parameters(fit).sd_phylo` is drmTMB's `sd_phylo_stable`. `ranef(fit)[:species_phylo]` holds the stable modes and `ranef(fit)[:species]` the OU modes. `simulate` and `bootstrap_ci` draw a fresh phylogenetic vector, fresh OU paths and fresh noise.
  - **Refusals.** drmTMB's pairing rules are refused by name: OU only; an unlabelled intercept on the same grouping; no ordinary `(1 | species)`; ≥ 3 species, each with ≥ 2 times; ≥ 3 distinct lags; tree tips exactly the observed species, with unobserved tips named.
  - **Parity.** Two drmTMB cells (draft #1448, final head `012258e9f`): a 24-species fixture and drmTMB's 60-species article data. On Julia 1.10.12: logLik ≤ 5.5e-12, every estimate ≤ 2.1e-11 relative, conditional fitted values ≤ 2.3e-12.
  - **No interval claim.** Only profile `confint` warns that the paired fit's intervals are uncalibrated, as in drmTMB. drmTMB refuses Wald and bootstrap intervals for this fit; DRModels.jl returns them as an extension, without a calibration claim, and does not warn that they are uncalibrated.
- **Temporal boundary diagnostic (twin of drmTMB's `temporal_boundary` and random-effect-SD checks, drmTMB #1447 / #1448).** Every `temporal()` fit (AR1, OU, paired phylo + OU) is checked against drmTMB's rules:
  - residual σ̂ below 1e-3 · sd(y);
  - OU decay × longest series span below 1e-4, or × shortest gap above 30;
  - AR1 |φ̂| above 0.999;
  - the smallest random-effect SD (process, phylogenetic or `(1 | id)`) below 1e-4.

  A fit that meets any rule warns at fit time (quietly inside bootstrap and profile refits) and reports `check_drm(fit).temporal_boundary = (at_boundary = true, findings = […])`. drmTMB calls this `convergence_status() == "boundary"`.
  - On drmTMB's own boundary panel, DRModels.jl reaches decay ≈ 1.4e-18 with drmTMB's β, σ and process SD. Its logLik, −45.5124249, equals a 256-bit dense evaluation; drmTMB prints −45.5124225 there, a precision loss at the boundary, not in DRModels.jl.
  - On a panel whose true stable SD is 0 (12 species × 5 times), the fitted phylogenetic SD (3.9e-10) is flagged.
  - **Caveat.** As in drmTMB, the σ rule uses the marginal sd(y), so it can fire when a covariate dominates the response.
