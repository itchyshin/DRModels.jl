Fix: `simulate(fit; re = :zero)` now really draws with the random effects set to zero on fits with an `sd()` formula on `sigma` (Gaussian, NB2, Gamma, BetaBinomial, structured sigma) and on the Gaussian joint mean-and-sigma route. Before, those fits stored the conditional `sigma` (and, on the joint route, the conditional mean), so the "zero random effect" draw still carried the fitted group modes: in the confirmed case the draw SD was 0.846 against a population sigma of 0.698. The numbers returned by `simulate(...; re = :zero)` on these fits change; all other fits are unchanged. The parametric-bootstrap fallback for fits without a fresh-draw simulator keeps its old conditional draw.
