- **Family markers for Poisson, Gamma, Beta, and LogNormal now reuse `Distributions.jl` types (#1534).** `using DRModels, Distributions` resolves one unqualified name for simulation and for `drm(..., Poisson())` (and the other three families). `Binomial` still clashes between the link-tagged DRM family and `Distributions.Binomial`; pass `Distributions.Binomial()` to `drm` for the default logit route, or `DRModels.Binomial(link = …)` for probit/cloglog.
