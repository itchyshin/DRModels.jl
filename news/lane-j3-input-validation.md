- **`drm` refuses inputs it used to drop or misread silently (#1027, #939, #938, #951, #1026, #1022, #1020, #1014, #1013, #1010, #966, #1017, #926, #974, #963).** Each refusal is an `ArgumentError` that names the argument: a formula for a parameter the family does not have (including `sigma ~ x` on `CumulativeLogit()`); `offset(...)` anywhere except the Poisson mean on the default marginal; `tree` / `K` / `A` / `coords` without the matching formula marker; `algorithm = :sparse` / `sparse = true` on a Gaussian model with no structured term; formula columns whose length differs from the response; phylo labels that are not tip names (they are no longer matched by order of appearance, and integer-valued Float64 codes are tip indices); asymmetric or non-finite bivariate `K` / `A`; negative or non-finite `meta_V` variances; univariate Gaussian `V = …`; and missing responses on the cross-family route. `drm_listwise` accepts `temporal()` formulas, and `r2_constant_sigma` refuses structured, `meta_V` and scale-random-effect fits and ignores dropped (NaN) rows.
- **The relatedness matrix `A` is matched to the data by label (#957).** Pass `A` as a table whose column names are the labels, or as a matrix with the new `ids = …` keyword, on the Gaussian, Poisson, NegBinomial2, Gamma, Beta and ZeroOneBeta `drm` methods. An unlabelled `A` keeps the first-appearance convention and warns once per session.
