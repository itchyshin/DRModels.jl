- **The simple Gaussian and Student-t fit no longer depends on the units of `y` (#936, twin of drmTMB #1751; owner ruling SHINICHI-0900-1119).**
  For fixed effects plus at most one ordinary `(1 | g)` on the mean, maximum likelihood, no `control` / `weights`, and `sd(y)` outside `[0.01, 100]`, `drm` fits `y / sd(y)` and maps the result back. Reported coefficients, the log SD of `(1 | g)`, standard errors, fitted means, random-effect estimates and `loglik` are on the original scale (`loglik = loglik* - n log sd(y)`, the same density as before). The convergence flags now see the same standardised problem at `y`, `1000 y` and `1e-6 y`. Every other route (slopes, structured effects, `sd()`, REML, offsets, weights, `control`, priors, missing responses, other families) is unchanged and not refused. Within `[0.01, 100]` nothing changes.
