- **`vc()` and `re_sd()` accept an intercept-only structured SD.** A Binomial `animal(1 | g)`, `relmat(1 | g)` or `spatial(1 | g)` fit, and a Beta `spatial(1 | g)` fit, keeps its one SD in a block named `:sd` with a single intercept coefficient. The two accessors read that as an SD regression and refused it with a message about `sd(group) ~ ...`. They now return the SD the fit used (and its 1 by 1 variance) when the `:sd` block is the intercept alone, and still refuse when it has covariates. On the Binomial animal example the SD agrees with drmTMB 0.8.1 (0.1649602). `heritability()` on such a fit now says that no structured variance component is available, instead of blaming SD covariates.
