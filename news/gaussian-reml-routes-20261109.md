- **`method = :REML` now fits eight more Gaussian mean-side designs that drmTMB 0.8.1 fits with `REML = TRUE`.** Slope-only `(0 + x | g)`; several independent scalar terms (`(1 | g) + (0 + x1 | g) + ...`, and crossed `(1 | g) + (1 | g2)`); one `relmat(1 | g)` or `animal(1 | g)`; and `phylo(1 + x | g)` with two SDs, each with a constant residual scale. The objective is the exact restricted likelihood (ML plus half the log-determinant of `X'V^-1X`, minus half `p log(2 pi)`), the same one the existing `(1 | g)` route uses. On three simulated datasets per group the REML logLik agrees with R to 1e-4 or better (see `docs/dev-log/2026-11-09-gaussian-reml-routes.md`). `spatial(1 | g)` and a correlated `(1 + x | g)` block beside another term stay refused, each with its own message.
