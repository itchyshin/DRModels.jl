- **`is_converged()` now rejects a Gaussian fit whose scale collapses at one exactly fitted observation (#1059).** A `sigma ~ x` fit can drive one observation's scale to zero while the location predictor passes through it, so the likelihood has no finite maximum while `Optim` reports convergence. The rule is unit-free: fitted scales spanning more than 10^4 together with a standardized residual below 10^-3 at the smallest-scale row. Fits with random effects, `meta_V`, or a non-Gaussian family are unchanged. Matches drmTMB#1469.
