- **Poisson `offset()` with one `(1 | g)` on `marginal = :Laplace` works again (#941, #938).** The input-validation guard added for #938 refused every `offset(...)` on `Poisson()` with `marginal = :Laplace`, on the belief that route never extracts the offset. It does: `_drm_ordinary_laplace` passes the offset to `_fit_poisson_ordinary_laplace` with its coefficient fixed at 1 (the #941 work, checked against drmTMB). The guard now admits `offset(...)` in the Poisson mean on every route and leaves the route-level refusals in place: no `(1 | g)`, a random slope, crossed or structured effects, `zi`/`hu` and `marginal = :VA` on Poisson, and any `offset(...)` on another family or in a non-mean formula, are still `ArgumentError`s or explicit errors, never a free coefficient.
