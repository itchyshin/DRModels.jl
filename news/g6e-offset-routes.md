- **Offsets: two offsets, ZIP and `meta_V` now match drmTMB.** `y ~ x + offset(a) + offset(b)` adds the two offsets, as drmTMB does (it was refused with "only one `offset(...)` term"); the rule applies to every route that accepts an offset. `Poisson()` with `zi ~ ...` accepts an offset on the mean (hurdle `hu` and `zi` with a random effect stay refused). Gaussian `meta_V(v)` with an offset on the mean fits as `y - offset` with the offset added back to the fitted means. On the gap-map cells, log-likelihood agrees with drmTMB 0.8.1 to the pinned 1e-4 (`test/test_offset_routes_parity.jl`).
