- **The aliased fixed-effect design check now runs on every univariate route.** A design with a column that is a linear combination of others (for example `x3 = x1 + x2`) was flagged only on the Gaussian location-scale route. On Binomial, Poisson and the other families the fit came back with `is_converged` true and a finite standard error on the aliased coefficient. It now behaves as the Gaussian route and as drmTMB does: a `fixed_effect_rank_deficiency` warning, `is_converged` false, the standard error of the aliased coefficient set to NaN. The log-likelihood and the other estimates are unchanged, and a full-rank design is untouched. The check reads the fixed-effect design of each formula part once before the fit; a formula or data it cannot read (for example a predictor with missing values) is left to the route, and bivariate and mixed-family fits are not covered yet.
