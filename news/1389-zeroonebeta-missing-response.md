- **`ZeroOneBeta()` fits with a missing response no longer stop with `KeyError: key :zoi not found`, and `predict_parameters()` / `predict(type = :mean)` work on a ZeroOneBeta fit that wrote no `zoi` or `coi` formula.** `bf()` keeps only the formulas the user wrote, so `predict_parameters` returned no `:zoi` or `:coi` for `bf(y ~ x)` although both are estimated (intercept only). The fit rebuilds its per-row parameters from that prediction after the optimiser finishes, so any response with a `missing` value failed there; `predict(fit, newdata; type = :mean)` failed the same way on complete data. `predict_parameters` now supplies the implied `zoi ~ 1` and `coi ~ 1`, equal to the fit with those formulas written out. The fixed-effect fits give `nobs = 50` and the drmTMB 0.8.1 log-likelihoods (2.533589 and 2.585723) on the issue's data; the random-intercept fit now runs.
