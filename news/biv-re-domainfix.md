- **Bivariate Gaussian `(1 | p | g)` no longer aborts on ordinary data.** On simulated data with small or unequal groups, about one fit in eight stopped with `DomainError: log was called with a negative real argument` (or `SingularException`), although drmTMB fits the same data. The line search tried a point with the residual correlation at its guard and a tiny residual SD. There the group-sum matrix is finite but numerically not positive definite, so `logdet` threw. Such a point is now rejected like an infeasible point (a large flat value, as on the sibling routes), and the line search backs off. A second kind of bad point, where the weighted residual sum of squares cancels to a large negative number, is rejected the same way. On 240 new simulated data sets (unbalanced groups, weak and strong group correlation, small SDs) the failure rate fell from 13.3% to 0.4% for ML and from 12.1% to 0% for REML. With the rejection of indefinite points alone, every fit that worked before was bit for bit unchanged (419 of 419 on Julia 1.13.3 and on 1.10.12). The last failure (one ML fit in 240 on each Julia version, and seed 7080 of a second set of 100) is also gone: it came from a line search that got the finite rejection value 1e18 at one trial and `Inf` (from an older non-finite exit) at the next, and its bracketing step asserts that values are finite. Every rejected trial on this route now returns the same 1e18. This moves 10 of 690 working fits on Julia 1.13.3 and 9 of 691 on 1.10.12 in the last digits (logLik by at most 2.7e-11). In 3 of them on 1.13.3 (2 on 1.10.12) the estimate moves by 0.1 to 1.7 along a flat boundary direction; each is a boundary fit, none changes its agreement class with drmTMB, and no fit that agreed with drmTMB stops agreeing.
