- **Bivariate Gaussian `(1 | p | g)` no longer aborts on ordinary data.** On simulated data with small or unequal groups, about one fit in eight stopped with `DomainError: log was called with a negative real argument` (or `SingularException`), although drmTMB fits the same data. The line search tried a point with the residual correlation at its guard and a tiny residual SD. There the group-sum matrix is finite but numerically not positive definite, so `logdet` threw. Such a point is now rejected like an infeasible point (a large flat value, as on the sibling routes), and the line search backs off. A second kind of bad point, where the weighted residual sum of squares cancels to a large negative number, is rejected the same way. On 240 new simulated data sets (unbalanced groups, weak and strong group correlation, small SDs) the failure rate fell from 13.3% to 0.4% for ML and from 12.1% to 0% for REML. Every fit that worked before is bit for bit unchanged (419 of 419 on Julia 1.13.3 and on 1.10.12). One ML fit in 240 still stops with an assertion from the line search. That is a different failure: the line search got the finite rejection value 1e18 at one trial and `Inf` (from an older non-finite exit) at the next, and its bracketing step asserts that values are finite. Using 1e18 for every rejection on this route would fix it, but it changes 8 of 211 fits that work today in the last digits, so it is left for the owner.
