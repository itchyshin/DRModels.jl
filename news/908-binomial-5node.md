- **`Binomial()` random intercept `(1 | g)`: the default integrator is now 5-node adaptive Gauss-Hermite (was 3) (#908).** This matches the other families. Fits can change slightly, always towards the true marginal likelihood. On `MASS::bacteria` (sparse binary, `y ~ week + (1 | ID)`), the 3-node default put `sd_mu` at 1.2866 (5.1% below the 61-node reference 1.3562, 0.22 nat of log-likelihood short); 5 nodes gives 1.3475 (0.64% below, 0.014 nat short). On the other binomial cells checked (binary and `cbind`), the change is below 1e-3 relative on `sd_mu` and 1e-3 nat on logLik. Binomial `(1 | g)` fits take roughly 1.6 times as many quadrature evaluations per group; wall time on the binomial test files is reported in the PR. Only the ordinary `(1 | g)` route changes: crossed, phylo, `(1 + x | g)` (already 5 nodes), `marginal = :Laplace` and `marginal = :VA` are untouched. Evidence: `docs/dev-log/evidence/binomial-5node/`.
