- **Prior weights on the bivariate Gaussian with `(1 | p | g)`.** `drm(bf(mu1 = y1 ~ x1 + (1 | p | g), mu2 = y2 ~ x2 + (1 | p | g), ...), Gaussian(); data, weights = w)` now fits, ML and REML, as drmTMB does. A weight multiplies the whole row (the joint density of both responses): an integer weight equals repeating the row, a zero weight equals dropping it, and `nobs` still counts rows. Rows with a missing response drop together with their weights. Measured against drmTMB 0.8.1 on 9 cells (log-likelihood within 1e-4, fixed effects within 1e-3 of the R SE, log SDs and correlation within 5e-3, standard errors within 1e-2). Negative, non-finite, missing, wrong-length or all-zero weights are refused. `update()` on a weighted fit of this kind now needs `weights =` stated (`weights = weights(fit)` to keep them), and the parametric bootstrap refits keep the weights.
