- **Fix: `repeatability`, `icc` and `heritability` no longer treat a random intercept on log σ as a variance component.** A `sigma ~ (1 | g)` random intercept (SD `re_sd(fit)[:g_logsigma]`) lives on the log σ scale. Before this fix it was counted as a response-scale variance: a fit with only `sigma ~ (1 | g)` returned a meaningless ratio, and a fit with `y ~ x + (1 | g)` and `sigma ~ (1 | g)` could not pick its mean component without `component =` and could even pick the log-σ SD. The ratios now use the mean components only, and a scale random intercept turns the residual entry into the marginal residual variance `E[σ²] = exp(2 b₀ + 2 Σ ω²)`, as drmTMB does. On the mean-plus-scale fixture DRModels.jl matches drmTMB's `repeatability()` estimate and SE to 1e-6 (0.2032 and 0.0471, drmTMB `0eb0467`); the interval differs slightly because DRModels.jl centres it on the bias-corrected estimate. A fit whose only random effect is on `sigma` is refused, as in drmTMB. The fit-time variance-boundary check made the same mistake (it added the log-σ modes to the response-scale structured total) and is fixed the same way.
