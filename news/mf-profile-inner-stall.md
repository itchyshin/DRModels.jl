- **Cross-family latent-rho profile CI no longer drops an arm to `NaN` when a penalised inner solve stalls a hair short of `g_tol`.** The first inner solve of the profile (far start, penalty 1e4, deviance far above the chi-square cutoff) can stop at `g_residual` ~ 1e-9 to 1e-7 without meeting `g_tol = 1e-9`, because trial objectives differ by less than the ~1e-12 noise floor at nll ~ 600. Whether it stalls depends on last-ulp differences (start point, CPU, BLAS kernel), so the same fit returned `(NaN, hi)` on some runners and not others (seen as a flaky CI failure on Julia 1.10). A stalled solve is now retried once from the stall point with a Hager-Zhang line search and accepted if stationary to 1e-6; solves that already converged, and sentinel (>= 1e9) solves, are unchanged.
