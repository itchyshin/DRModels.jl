- **NegBinomial2 `spatial(1 | site)` now accepts `coords` without an SD formula.** The fit uses drmTMB's fixed-range kernel, `exp(-d / r) + 1e-6 I` with `r` the median positive pairwise distance, as the Poisson and Gamma coordinate routes do. On a reference cell it agrees with drmTMB 0.8.1: log-likelihood to 4e-10, fixed effects within 1e-9 R standard errors, log SD to 5e-7 (`test/test_nb2_spatial_intercept_coords.jl`). Before this the call was refused with "needs either a precomputed spatial covariance via `K`". A supplied `K` behaves as before.
- **`NegBinomial2()` refuses a second structured term instead of dropping it.** A formula such as `spatial(1 | g) + animal(1 | h)`, `phylo(1 | g) + relmat(1 | h)` or `spatial(1 | g) + spatial(1 | h)` on the NB2 mean used to be fitted with the first structured term only, and the fit was returned as if both terms had been fitted. The call now stops with an `ArgumentError` that names the terms. The test covers all 16 ordered pairs of `phylo`, `relmat`, `animal` and `spatial`. The same refusal applies when the formula also has an `sd()` formula, for example `sd(g, spatial) ~ z` with `coords`, where the second term used to be dropped without a message. Fits with one structured term are unchanged.
