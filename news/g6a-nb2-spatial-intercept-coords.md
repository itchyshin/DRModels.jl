- **NegBinomial2 `spatial(1 | site)` now accepts `coords` without an SD formula.** The fit uses drmTMB's fixed-range kernel, `exp(-d / r) + 1e-6 I` with `r` the median positive pairwise distance, as the Poisson and Gamma coordinate routes do. On the wave-8 gap-map cell it agrees with drmTMB 0.8.1: log-likelihood to 4e-10, fixed effects within 1e-9 R standard errors, log SD to 5e-7 (`test/test_nb2_spatial_intercept_coords.jl`). Before this the call was refused with "needs either a precomputed spatial covariance via `K`". A supplied `K` behaves as before.
