Change: `update(fit, formula; data)` on a fit made with prior `weights` now throws an `ArgumentError` unless `weights =` is passed again. Before, the refit silently dropped the weights. Pass one weight per row of `data` (recommended), or `weights = nothing` to refit unweighted on purpose. When no row was dropped from the fit, `weights = weights(fit)` is that vector; when rows with a missing value were dropped, `weights(fit)` is shorter than `data`, so pass your original vector. Fits without weights are unchanged. drmTMB keeps the weights because it re-runs the original call; DRModels cannot, because `update` takes new `data`.
