Change: `update(fit, formula; data)` on a fit made with prior `weights` now throws an `ArgumentError` unless `weights =` is passed again. Before, the refit silently dropped the weights. Pass `weights = weights(fit)` for the same rows, new weights for new rows, or `weights = nothing` to refit unweighted on purpose. Fits without weights are unchanged. drmTMB keeps the weights because it re-runs the original call; DRModels cannot, because `update` takes new `data`.
