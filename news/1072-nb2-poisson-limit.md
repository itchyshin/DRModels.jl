- **NB2 at the Poisson limit is flagged, and no longer stops short of an interior optimum (#1072, #1069, #1039).** With no overdispersion the NB2 maximum-likelihood estimate is the Poisson limit (σ → 0). The fixed-effects and default `(1 | g)` fits now warn there (`converged` unchanged), refit once from an interior σ when they land on that plateau (keeping the refit only if it is interior with a lower nll), and fall back from the Hager–Zhang line search to BackTracking and then Nelder–Mead if the search asserts. On the #1069 seeds the `(1 | g)` fit now matches drmTMB's interior σ (seed 20282003: log σ −3.791, +1.46 log-likelihood units over the Poisson GLMM). The sparse-Laplace routes (`phylo`, `relmat`/`animal`/`spatial`, crossed intercepts and the Laplace start of crossed `marginal = :AGHQ`) cap the size at `exp(8)` (log σ ≥ −4); there the refit triggers at that cap. On a simulated Poisson crossed design, where the optimiser had stopped on the cap, the refit recovers an interior optimum (log σ −2.64) 1.8 log-likelihood units better than the best fit on the cap. A fit that stays at the cap gets an NB2-specific Poisson-limit warning in place of the generic #959 clamp line, and the AGHQ route no longer repeats it for its Laplace start. Not covered: the correlated-slope, zero-inflated, hurdle, truncated, VA, `marginal = :Laplace`, coupled location–scale and mixed-family NB2 routes; location standard errors with σ held at the limit (#1072 request 3).
