- **`converged` on the bivariate phylogenetic and coevolution routes now requires the gradient (#946).** The q=4 ML, Fisher-z and REML fitters and the q=2 / general-q coevolution fitters (ML and REML) stop the optimiser on a relative change of the objective, and used to report Optim's own flag, which is true at such a plateau whatever the gradient. `converged` is now true only when the gradient re-evaluated at the reported point meets the route's `g_tol`, or the unit-free Newton decrement (#983) is negligible, and never on an iteration- or time-limit stop. Estimates are unchanged. Fits that stopped short of the optimum now report `converged = false`. On the Anolis q=2 data from #944, base reported `true` at logLik 76.993295 (ML) and 73.419352 (REML); drmTMB reaches 76.993609 and 73.441676 and calls both degenerate. A finite-difference probe that is not finite no longer counts as a zero gradient, and the coevolution fitters return `g_residual`. Each route gains an `f_reltol` keyword; its default is the old constant.
