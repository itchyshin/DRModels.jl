- **`poly(x, d)` now works inside a Julia `@formula`, with R's orthogonal basis.** `@formula(y ~ poly(x1, 2))` used to fail with `UndefVarError: poly` before DRModels ran (eight gap-map v2 cells). `DRModels` now exports `poly`; the columns, the coefficient names (`poly(x1, 2)1`, `poly(x1, 2)2`) and the log-likelihood match drmTMB 0.8.1 for Gaussian, Poisson, Binomial, Gamma, Beta, NegBinomial2, LogNormal and Student (`test/test_poly_term.jl`, reference `test/fixtures/poly_parity/`). The basis comes from the complete rows of the fitting data, as in R, and is stored so `predict(fit, newdata)` rebuilds the training basis at the new rows. Only `poly(column, degree)` is accepted; a transformed argument, `raw = TRUE`, several variables and `coefs =` are refused with a message. A formula object keeps the basis of its first fit, so a refit with the same `bf(...)` on other data reuses it (same model, different coefficients); build a new `bf(...)` for a fresh basis. The R double bar `(1 + x1 || g)` is unchanged: Julia's parser rejects `||` before DRModels sees it, so keep writing `(1 | g) + (0 + x1 | g)`.
