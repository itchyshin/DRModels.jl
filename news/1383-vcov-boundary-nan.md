- **`vcov` no longer holds a negative variance at a flagged boundary coordinate (#1383).** When the Hessian guard flags a coordinate as numerically flat (for example a random-effect SD at zero), that coordinate is dropped before the rest of the Hessian is inverted, and its row and column of `vcov(fit)` are `NaN`. Before, the pseudo-inverse inverted finite-difference noise there and could return a variance of -1e8 (a Binomial `(1 | g)` fit with `marginal = :Laplace` on 20 rows), so `sqrt.(diag(vcov(fit)))` threw a `DomainError`. The other coordinates keep their covariances, and `bridge_diagnostics(fit).boundary` lists the flagged ones. `stderror` is `NaN` at a flagged coordinate (not computed), where it was `Inf` or, for a large positive variance, a finite number (3153 on the bivariate structured fit with a rank-one among-group covariance); the Wald interval of that coordinate stays `(-Inf, Inf)`. A covariance that is `NaN` throughout (`se = false`, a failed fit, every coordinate flagged) still gives `Inf`. `predict(...; se = true)` and `predict_parameters`, `bias_correct`, the cross-family correlation interval, and `repeatability`, `icc` and `heritability` with `newdata` keep their standard errors for a row or quantity that does not use the flagged coordinate; one that does gets `NaN`. Fits with no flagged coordinate are unchanged.

  Three side effects to know about. (1) `convergence_status` changes from `:degenerate` to `:boundary` on two fits: the 20-row Binomial `cbind(s, fl) ~ x + (1 | g)` fit with `marginal = :Laplace`, and the 20-row Poisson `y ~ x + (1 | g)` fit with `marginal = :Laplace`; a third fit with a flagged coordinate (the Binomial default quadrature fit on the same data) stays `:degenerate`. (2) `issymmetric(vcov(fit))` is `false` for a fit with a flagged coordinate (`NaN != NaN`), and `eigvals(Symmetric(vcov(fit)))` throws `ArgumentError: matrix contains Infs or NaNs`; call them on the unflagged block, `V[keep, keep]` with `keep = setdiff(axes(V, 1), bridge_diagnostics(fit).boundary)`. When every coordinate is flagged, `vcov` is now all `NaN` (main returned all zeros). (3) `repeatability`, `icc` and `heritability` with `newdata` (`src/heritability.jl`) lost the standard error of every row when one coordinate was `NaN`, because `0 * NaN` is `NaN`; a row that does not use the flagged coordinate now keeps its standard error.
