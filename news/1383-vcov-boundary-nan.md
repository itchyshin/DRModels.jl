- **`vcov` no longer holds a negative variance at a flagged boundary coordinate (#1383).** When the Hessian guard flags a coordinate as numerically flat (for example a random-effect SD at zero), that coordinate is now dropped before the rest of the Hessian is inverted, and its row and column of `vcov(fit)` are `NaN`. Before, the pseudo-inverse inverted finite-difference noise there and could return a variance of -1e8 (a Binomial `(1 | g)` fit with `marginal = :Laplace` on 20 rows), so `sqrt.(diag(vcov(fit)))` threw a `DomainError`. The unflagged coordinates keep their covariances; `stderror` still shows `Inf` for the flagged one, and some such fits now report `convergence_status = :boundary` instead of `:degenerate`. `bias_correct` keeps its standard error for a quantity that does not involve the flagged coordinate. Fits with no flagged coordinate are unchanged.
