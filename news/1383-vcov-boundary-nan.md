- **`vcov` and `stderror` give `NaN`, not a made-up number, at a coordinate the fit flags as a boundary (#1383).** When the Hessian guard flags a coordinate as numerically flat (for example a random-effect SD at zero, or the size of a negative binomial fitted to Poisson data), that coordinate is dropped before the rest of the Hessian is inverted, and its row and column of `vcov(fit)` are `NaN`. Before, the pseudo-inverse inverted finite-difference noise there and could return a variance of -1e8 (a Binomial `(1 | g)` fit with `marginal = :Laplace` on 20 rows), so `sqrt.(diag(vcov(fit)))` threw a `DomainError`. The other coordinates keep their covariances, and `bridge_diagnostics(fit).boundary` lists the flagged ones. `stderror` is `NaN` at a flagged coordinate (it was `Inf`, or a finite number such as 3153 on the bivariate structured fit with a rank-one among-group covariance), and so is its Wald interval and its entry in `coeftable`. When every coordinate is flagged, every standard error is `NaN`; the fit stores that flag (`uncertainty` is `:boundary_all`), so it stays different from `se = false` and from a failed covariance, which still give `Inf`. `bias_correct` of a quantity that uses a flagged coordinate gives `NaN` (not `Inf`). `predict(...; se = true)`, `predict_parameters`, `bias_correct`, the cross-family correlation interval, and `repeatability`, `icc` and `heritability` with `newdata` keep their standard errors for a row or quantity that does not use the flagged coordinate; one that does gets `NaN`. Fits with no flagged coordinate are unchanged.

  **The flag does not depend on units.** A coefficient on a covariate, a location coefficient of a Gaussian, Student or skew-normal fit (units of the response), and an off-diagonal covariance entry carry units, so their curvature changes with the units of the data. They are no longer flagged for being small next to another coordinate; they are flagged only when their curvature is exactly zero. Every other coordinate (log SDs, log sizes, correlations, intercepts of log and logit blocks) is flagged when its curvature is at most 3e-8 times the largest curvature among the others of that kind (the number of observations when there is only one). The `drm` entry applies this once the fit is built, and it can only remove a flag that the matrix alone would have set. A well identified Gaussian fit with a covariate multiplied by 1e5 used to get `NaN` standard errors and status `:degenerate` (and a warning about a variance boundary); it now gets finite standard errors that scale exactly with the covariate, no warning, and `:converged`. Called on a bare matrix, `DRModels._vcov_from_hessian` still compares each curvature with the largest one.

  Four side effects to know about. (1) `convergence_status` changes from `:degenerate` to `:boundary` for a fit whose flagged coordinates are all random-effect log SDs at or below the sparse-Laplace floor of -8 (a `:resd` block), the same case the floor rule of #1190 already handles, when the old pseudo-inverse had left a non-positive or infinite variance there: the 20-row Binomial `cbind(s, fl) ~ x + (1 | g)` fit with `marginal = :Laplace`, and the 20-row Poisson `y ~ x + (1 | g)` fit with `marginal = :Laplace`. Any other flagged coordinate leaves the status at `:degenerate`, because the covariance is then not finite. (2) `issymmetric(vcov(fit))` is `false` for a fit with a flagged coordinate (`NaN != NaN`), and `eigvals(Symmetric(vcov(fit)))` throws `ArgumentError: matrix contains Infs or NaNs`; call them on the unflagged block, `V[keep, keep]` with `keep = setdiff(axes(V, 1), bridge_diagnostics(fit).boundary)`. When every coordinate is flagged, `vcov` is now all `NaN` (it was all zeros). (3) `repeatability`, `icc` and `heritability` with `newdata` (`src/heritability.jl`) lost the standard error of every row when one coordinate was `NaN`, because `0 * NaN` is `NaN`; a row that does not use the flagged coordinate now keeps its standard error. (4) The status still applies the drmTMB rule on inflated standard errors, so a covariate in units of 1e-5 (a slope standard error in the thousands) gives `:degenerate` without any flagged coordinate, as in drmTMB.
