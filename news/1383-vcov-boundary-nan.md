- **`vcov` no longer holds a negative variance at a flagged boundary coordinate (#1383).** When the Hessian guard flags a coordinate as numerically flat (for example a random-effect SD at zero), that coordinate is now dropped before the rest of the Hessian is inverted, and its row and column of `vcov(fit)` are `NaN`. Before, the pseudo-inverse inverted finite-difference noise there and could return a variance of -1e8 (a Binomial `(1 | g)` fit with `marginal = :Laplace` on 20 rows), so `sqrt.(diag(vcov(fit)))` threw a `DomainError`. The unflagged coordinates keep their covariances; `stderror` shows `Inf` for the flagged one (a large positive variance there, which main could return, now also shows `Inf`), and some such fits now report `convergence_status = :boundary` instead of `:degenerate`. `issymmetric(vcov(fit))` is `false` and `eigvals(Symmetric(vcov(fit)))` throws for such a fit; use the unflagged sub-block. `bias_correct`, and `repeatability`, `icc` and `heritability` with `newdata`, keep their standard errors for a quantity that does not involve the flagged coordinate. Fits with no flagged coordinate are unchanged.
