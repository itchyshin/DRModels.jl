- **New: `TruncatedPoisson()` family (#726, owner decision 16a).** Zero-truncated Poisson for strictly-positive counts, mirroring `TruncatedNegBinomial2()` and drmTMB's `truncated_poisson()`. `TruncatedPoisson()` + `hu ~ ...` is the shared twin spelling of the hurdle Poisson: it delegates to the existing `Poisson()` + `hu` route and gives bit-identical fits (the fit reports `Poisson` as its family). `Poisson()` + `hu` keeps working. Without `hu` it fits the plain zero-truncated Poisson (fixed effects only); `zi`, `sigma`, random effects and zeros in the response are refused. Bridge name `truncated_poisson`.
