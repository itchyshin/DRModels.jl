- **Gaussian REML location standard errors now match drmTMB when `sigma` has a regression.** On the fixed-effect Gaussian location-scale route with `method = :REML` and `sigma ~ x` (or any `sigma` formula beyond an intercept), `stderror`, `vcov`, `coeftable` and `confint` (Wald) for the location coefficients now add the term for the uncertainty in the `sigma` coefficients, and `vcov` carries the location-`sigma` cross block, as drmTMB does. Example (24 rows, `y ~ x`, `sigma ~ x`): location SEs were 0.25290 and 0.30365 and are now 0.25409 and 0.30537 (drmTMB: 0.25409 and 0.30537), a rise of 0.47 and 0.57 percent. With `sigma ~ 1` nothing changes beyond rounding. Estimates, log-likelihoods, the `sigma` SEs and every maximum-likelihood fit are unchanged, and so is the REML route with random effects, which already matched.
