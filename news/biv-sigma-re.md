- **Bivariate Gaussian with a correlated random intercept pair on the two scales.** `bf(mu1 = y1 ~ x1, mu2 = y2 ~ x2, sigma1 = ~ 1 + (1 | p | g), sigma2 = ~ 1 + (1 | p | g), rho12 = ~ 1)` with `Gaussian()` now fits, as drmTMB does: each group shifts log sigma1 and log sigma2 by a correlated pair `u_g ~ N(0, Sigma_g)`, estimated by the Laplace approximation (ML only). Covariates on `mu1`, `mu2`, the scale formulas and `rho12` are allowed. The group covariance is reported as in the mean-side route (the `:groupcov` block, `fit.ranef.Sigma_g`); `fit.scales` holds the scale without the group effects, and the conditional scale of a row is `fit.scales[:sigma1][i] * exp(u[1, g_i])`. `simulate(fit; data)` and the bootstrap redraw the group effects. Measured against drmTMB 0.8.1 on four cells: log-likelihood within 1e-4, fixed effects within 1e-3 of the R SE, log SDs and the transformed correlation within 5e-3 on the three interior cells. On the gap-map cell drmTMB itself sits on a boundary (group correlation 1) and the same boundary mode is reached. Still refused with a clear message: a random effect on the means together with one on the scales, REML, `weights`, partly observed rows, `(1 + x | p | g)`, one-sided or unlabelled terms. The fit is the best of three starts for the group covariance. A start that stops with a numerical error is skipped with a warning, and the fit fails only if all three fail (one simulated data set, 60 rows in 12 unbalanced groups, used to abort on the first failing start; it now fits and matches drmTMB). The best of three is not a global search: on some small data sets a better mode exists that none of the three starts reaches, and drmTMB misses it too. The refusal text for slope terms, the Student-t family and unlabelled terms no longer says that the supported `(1 | p | g)` model is unsupported; it now names the one supported shape.
