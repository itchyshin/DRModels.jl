## Regressions (the check that matters)

Rules were the wave-8 pre-registered ones, with wave 8 as the base: Julia fit to not-fit; agreeing to disagreeing; refusal to crash; any new crash type. **All four rules: 0 cells.** I also flagged any fitted cell whose logLik moved by more than 1e-6. That flag fires on 3 cells, below.

### Cells that changed without flipping (3)

All three are `binomial|indep|lab0|q{2,3,4}|sd-z|cm0|ML`, which fit and disagreed with R at wave 8 and still do. Julia's logLik rose at q = 2 and q = 3, so these are the effect of the wave-10 restarts, not a loss.

| Cell | R logLik | Wave 8 Julia | Wave 10 Julia | Julia minus R, wave 8 to wave 10 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| q = 2 | -253.529 | -255.224 | -253.145 | -1.69 to +0.38 |
| q = 3 | -253.529 | -254.560 | -253.128 | -1.03 to +0.40 |
| q = 4 | -250.138 | -249.558 | -249.144 | +0.58 to +0.99 |

At q = 4 Julia was already above R at wave 8 and moved further above it. Julia above R means a different or better optimum than R reached, or a different integrator (Julia defaults to adaptive Gauss-Hermite, R uses Laplace); I did not separate these. The gap to R got larger at q = 4 (0.58 to 0.99) and smaller at q = 2 and 3. Treat q = 4 as a number to look at, not as a regression, because the likelihood went up.

## Method and compute

Same grid, data, runners and classifier as the wave-8 map (unchanged copies of `run_julia.jl`, `driver.py`, `classify.py`, `cells*.tsv`, `data/`). Julia 1.10.12 on Totoro, `Pkg.develop` of a `git archive` of the tip, depot copied from `~/drm_w10_20261109` (the original untouched), 40 workers, one thread per process, `nice -n 10`, `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1`, `JULIA_NUM_THREADS=1`. R was not rerun: the stored R results from the wave-8 branch were reused, because the R library and data did not change. Estimate before launching: 15 to 30 minutes of Julia (wave 8 took 14.9 min for two tips at 20 workers each). Actual: 11 minutes wall time from launch to the last cell, of which the last 6 minutes were one timed-out cell. No process of mine remains on Totoro. Classifier output: `maps/`; raw runner output: `raw-julia/`.

Reproduce: `run_wave10.sh` (paths are the Totoro paths used), `python3 -I classify.py <raw dir with r/ and julia/> <out>` from the wave-8 harness, then `python3 -I compare10.py <wave-8 gap_map.tsv> <wave-10 gap_map.tsv> <out>`.

## Not verified

- R side was reused, not rerun. I did not diff it against a fresh R run on this date.
- The wave-8 numbers come from the stored wave-8 map, not from a wave-8 Julia rerun here.
- The 20 new Julia-ahead fits and the 32 flips were checked only through logLik (flips agree to 1e-10). Estimates, SEs and intervals were not compared.
- Effort and "what Julia needs" in the ranking are inferred from the refusal text and the family pattern. I did not read the Julia source for each route.
- The 54 logLik disagreements were not re-analysed; wave 8's attribution (adaptive Gauss-Hermite against Laplace) was not retested. Whether the three binomial `indep sd-z` cells moved toward a better optimum or only a different integrator was not separated.
- Spatial `coords` were passed only as a Dict (no matrix-coords pass), as in the wave-8 headline map; wave 8 found the matrix form changed no spatial refusal.
- Wave 9's own comparison script (`tools/gaussian_sd_gaps_compare.jl`) covers a different cell set (Gaussian SD gaps against a stored reference) and was not reused; the harness here is the wave-8 fuzz harness.
